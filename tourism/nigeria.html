01

Featured destinations

Where the country actually takes you.

Mangrove to Sahel

Niger delta mangrove in the south, rainforest, savanna, and the Sahel at the Niger border.

The Obudu Plateau

Sixteen hundred metres of grassland on the Cameroon border, with cattle ranches and cloud.

Erin-Ijesha and Gurara

Erin-Ijesha's seven stages in Osun, and the Gurara falls on the road to Abuja.

The Niger and the Benue

Two great rivers meeting at Lokoja, and the delta they build a thousand kilometres downstream.

Lagos and the Atlantic

Tarkwa Bay, Elegushi and the long Atlantic beaches east towards Lekki.

Cross River

The Cross River forest holds the last of Nigeria's gorillas and some of West Africa's tallest trees.

Wildlife & safari

What lives here, and how you see it without disturbing it.

Wildlife

Yankari and Gashaka

Yankari's elephant herds, and Gashaka-Gumti's forest chimpanzees on the Cameroon border.

Yankari

The country's best game reserve, with a warm spring you can swim in at the centre of it.

Community Forest

Afi Mountain's drill ranch and the Cross River community forests, both village-run.

03

Culture & heritage

The part of a country you cannot photograph from a vehicle.

Culture

Five Hundred Languages

Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa and around five hundred more, in one country.

Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Fulani

Four large cultures and hundreds of smaller ones, each with its own courts and calendars.

Jollof, Suya and Egusi

Suya off the grill at night, egusi with pounded yam, and the jollof question again.

Osun-Osogbo and Calabar

The Osun-Osogbo grove festival in August, and the Calabar carnival through December.

Bronze, Adire and Aso Oke

Benin bronze casting still practised in Igun street, and adire resist-dyed cloth in Abeokuta.

Benin City and Sungbo's Eredo

The walls of Benin, and Sungbo's Eredo — a hundred and sixty kilometres of earthworks.

The Bronzes

The Benin bronzes are the reason half the museums of Europe have a Nigeria problem.

Lagos, Abuja, Kano

Lagos on the lagoon, purpose-built Abuja in the middle, and Kano's thousand-year-old walls.

Mud Mosques and Modernism

Hausa mud architecture in the north, and the 1970s modernism of Lagos and Abuja.

Danfo and Buka

The yellow buses, the roadside buka, and a city that does not stop at any hour.

Loud, and Welcoming

Not a quiet holiday — but there is nowhere friendlier once you are through the door.

Plateau and Delta

Hike, Paddle, Ride

The Jos plateau, the Obudu ranch trails, and creek boats through the delta.

Lagos Island Hotels

Victoria Island and Ikoyi at one end, and Obudu's mountain resort at the other.
