Carthage on the bay of Tunis, Roman Dougga and El Jem, the Sahara at Douz and Tozeur, and a Mediterranean coast that has been in the tourism business for sixty years.

01

Featured destinations

Where the country actually takes you.

Coast to Chott

The Cap Bon coast, the olive Sahel, and the salt flats of the Chott el Djerid.

The Dorsale

The Atlas tail crosses the north — Jebel Chambi is 1,544 metres and the highest point.

The Oasis Cascades

Chebika, Tamerza and Mides — the mountain oases of the west, with water in a dry country.

The Medjerda

The only permanent river, running out of Algeria and across the northern plain.

The Mediterranean

Hammamet, Djerba and Cap Bon — twelve hundred kilometres of it, and warm from May.

Cork Oak in the North

The Kroumirie forest of cork oak in the north-west, which surprises everyone.

02

Wildlife & safari

What lives here, and how you see it without disturbing it.

Wildlife

Ichkeul and the Desert

Ichkeul's wintering waterfowl, and the desert fox and gazelle of the south.

Not a Safari Country

The wildlife here is birds and desert species, and Ichkeul is the reason to bring binoculars.

Oasis Water

The oasis irrigation of Tozeur and Nefta, managing water in the driest place in the country.

03

Culture & heritage

The part of a country you cannot photograph from a vehicle.

Culture

Punic, Roman, Arab, Berber

Four layers, all of them still visible, and often in the same town.

The Berber South

The ksour of Tataouine, and the underground houses of Matmata.

Couscous, Brik and Harissa

Friday couscous, brik straight out of the pan, and harissa on everything.

Carthage and the Sahara Festival

The Carthage film and music festival in summer, and the Douz Sahara festival in December.

Mosaic, Ceramic and Silver

Roman mosaic technique still taught, Nabeul ceramic, and Berber silver from the south.

Carthage, Dougga, El Jem

El Jem's amphitheatre seats thirty-five thousand and is standing almost complete.

The Medinas

Tunis, Sousse and Kairouan — Kairouan's mosque is among the oldest in the Maghreb.

04

Cities & everyday life

Where people actually live between the landmarks.

Tunis and Sfax

Tunis at the head of its lake, and Sfax's walled medina working as a medina still.

Whitewash and Blue

Sidi Bou Said's blue and white, and the vaulted ksour of the desert south.

The Souk and the Café

Mint tea with pine nuts, and a souk that still mostly sells to the people who live there.

Two Hours from Europe

Short flights, shallow warm sea, and resorts that have been doing families for decades.

Adventure & outdoors

Adventure

Desert, Diving, Dunes

Camel treks from Douz, dune camps on the erg, and diving off Tabarka.

Desert and Dive

Dune trekking from Douz, quad routes on the chott, and diving at Tabarka.

Riad, Dar and Dune Camp

Restored dar houses in the medinas, and desert camps beyond the last palm.
