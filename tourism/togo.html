Koutammakou

A narrow strip from the Atlantic to the Sahel: palm coast, the Kloto hills with the country's best walking, and the fortified Batammariba houses at Koutammakou that are a World Heritage site.

01

Featured destinations

Where the country actually takes you.

The Kloto Hills

Coffee and cocoa country in the southwest, forested and cool, around Kpalime.

Mont Agou

986 metres, the highest point, walkable in a morning from Kpalime.

Kpime and Womé

Two falls in the Kloto hills, both of them a short walk off the Kpalime road.

Lake Togo and the Mono

A shallow lagoon behind the coast, and the Mono river marking the Benin border.

Fifty Kilometres of Coast

Palm-backed Atlantic sand from Lome east to Aneho, with a heavy swell.

The Kloto Forest

Semi-deciduous forest above Kpalime, and one of the richest butterfly sites in West Africa.

02

Wildlife & safari

What lives here, and how you see it without disturbing it.

Wildlife

Fazao-Malfakassa

Elephant, buffalo and a great many butterflies in the largest park in the country.

Small and Recovering

The parks here are being rebuilt after decades of pressure, and the wildlife with them.

Butterflies at Kloto

Over five hundred species recorded in one forest, and a local guiding tradition around them.

03

Culture & heritage

The part of a country you cannot photograph from a vehicle.

Culture

Ewe, Kabye, Tem, Batammariba

Ewe in the south, Kabye in the centre, Batammariba in the far northwest.

The Batammariba

Their name means 'those who shape the earth', and the houses are the argument.

Fufu and Gboma Dessi

Fufu with groundnut sauce, and gboma dessi — spinach stew with fish.

Evala and the Voodoo Market

Kabye wrestling in July, and the Akodessewa fetish market in Lome all year.

Batik and Pottery

Batik and wood carving around Kpalime, and pottery from the north.

Aneho and the Old Coast

The first colonial capital, with German and Afro-Brazilian buildings going quietly to seed.

Koutammakou, Listed

The Batammariba landscape has been a World Heritage site since 2004, and is still lived in.

04

Cities & everyday life

Where people actually live between the landmarks.

Lome

The only capital on a border, with the biggest market in the region behind the beach.

Towers of Mud

Takienta houses with granaries on top, and German colonial stone in Lome and Aneho.

The Grand Marche and the Nana Benz

The women who built the wax-print trade ran it from this market, and still do.

Beach and Waterfall in a Day

Togo is narrow enough that the coast and the Kloto hills are the same short trip.

Adventure

Walking Around Kpalime

Waterfalls, coffee farms, Mont Agou and the butterfly forest, all within an hour.

Mont Agou at Dawn

Up before six, and Ghana visible across the valley by seven.

A Handful

Beach hotels at Lome and a few good places at Kpalime.
