Zakouma

Zakouma brought its elephants back from four hundred and fifty to over six hundred, the Ennedi is a sandstone massif full of rock art and desert crocodiles, and the Ounianga lakes sit in the middle of the Sahara without a river feeding them.

Zakouma, and the lakes of OuniangaKnown for
01

Featured destinations

Where the country actually takes you.

The Ennedi

A sandstone massif eroded into arches, towers and canyons, with water at the bottom of them.

Emi Koussi and the Tibesti

A 3,415-metre volcano in the far north, the highest point in the Sahara.

None, and Guelta Instead

No falls. What the Ennedi has is the Guelta d'Archei, a permanent gorge pool.

Lake Chad and the Ounianga Lakes

A shrinking inland sea in the west, and eighteen lakes in the Sahara that no river feeds.

Landlocked, and the Lake

The beaches here are the shores of Lake Chad, worked by fishermen in reed boats.

Gallery Forest on the Rivers

The Chari and Logone carry woodland far north into the Sahel.

02

Wildlife & safari

What lives here, and how you see it without disturbing it.

Wildlife

Buffalo in Thousands

Zakouma's buffalo move in herds of over a thousand, and the Kordofan giraffe with them.

Zakouma in March

Everything concentrates on the last pans, and the birdlife with it — crowned crane in their thousands.

The Turnaround at Zakouma

African Parks took it on in 2010 after poaching had taken ninety per cent of the elephants.

03

Culture & heritage

The part of a country you cannot photograph from a vehicle.

Culture

Two Hundred Groups

Toubou and Zaghawa in the north, Sara in the south, and Arabic and French across both.

The Toubou

A desert people of the Tibesti and Ennedi, and among the most self-sufficient anywhere.

Boule and Grilled Capitaine

Millet boule with sauce, and Nile perch off the Chari grilled on the bank.

The Gerewol and the Camel Races

Wodaabe gatherings in the Sahel, and camel racing in the desert north.

Leatherwork and Silver

Toubou leather, Sara basketry, and silver from the N'Djamena market.

Ennedi Rock Art

Thousands of painted and engraved panels recording a Sahara with cattle and giraffe in it.

Two Sites in the Sahara

The Ennedi massif and the Ounianga lakes, both World Heritage, both barely visited.

04

Cities & everyday life

Where people actually live between the landmarks.

N'Djamena

A capital on the Chari, opposite Cameroon, with the largest market in the region.

Mud and Banco

Sahelian mud-brick in the towns, and reed and mat construction on the lake.

Tea in the Shade

Three glasses, poured from a height, and no business concluded before the third.

Serious Country

Distances are enormous and facilities are few. Zakouma is possible; the Ennedi is an expedition.

05

Adventure & outdoors

For travellers who would rather walk it than watch it.

Adventure

Crossing the Ennedi

Ten days by four-wheel-drive between arches and gueltas, sleeping under the rock.

Walking Zakouma

Zakouma allows walking with armed rangers, which very few West and Central African parks do.

One Camp, and It Is Good

Zakouma has a single seasonal camp. The Ennedi is tents you bring with you.

06

Travel inspiration

The frames people come home with.

Sandstone and Shadow

The Ennedi arches in the last hour, when the sandstone goes from orange to red.

The Guelta d'Archei

Camels come down a gorge to drink, and desert crocodiles live in the pool below them.
