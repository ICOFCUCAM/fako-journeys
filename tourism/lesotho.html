The Kingdom in the Sky

The only country entirely above a thousand metres, with Africa's highest single-drop waterfall at Maletsunyane, southern Africa's only ski resort, and dinosaur footprints in the sandstone.

01

Featured destinations

Where the country actually takes you.

The Maloti

Basalt highlands cut by river gorges, and the roof of southern Africa.

Thabana Ntlenyana

3,482 metres, the highest point in Africa south of Kilimanjaro.

Maletsunyane

192 metres in a single drop, and a commercial abseil down beside it.

The Senqu

The Orange river rises here and runs the whole width of South Africa afterwards.

High Reservoirs

Landlocked and mountainous: the water here is Katse and Mohale, and it is sold to South Africa.

Almost None

Above the treeline for most of the country, and the small patches left are protected.

02

Wildlife & safari

What lives here, and how you see it without disturbing it.

Wildlife

Bearded Vulture and Eland

The lammergeier breeds on the Drakensberg escarpment, and eland are back in the reserves.

Not the Business Here

This is a mountain country, not a savanna one. What you go for is the ground and the horses.

Water as the Economy

The Highlands Water Project sells water to South Africa, and the dams are the landscape now.

03

Culture & heritage

The part of a country you cannot photograph from a vehicle.

Culture

The Blanket and the Hat

The Basotho blanket is worn seriously and read closely, and the mokorotlo is on the flag.

The Basotho

One people, one language, and a kingdom that kept its independence by going uphill.

Papa, Moroho and Motoho

Maize papa with wild greens, and motoho — fermented sorghum drink.

Morija Arts and Cultural Festival

Music, poetry and horse racing at Morija every year, and the country turns up.

Blankets, Hats and Tapestry

Woven mohair tapestry, the conical mokorotlo hat, and the blankets themselves.

Thaba Bosiu

The flat-topped mountain Moshoeshoe I defended, and where the nation was made.

Maloti-Drakensberg

A World Heritage site shared with South Africa, for its landscape and its San rock art.

04

Cities & everyday life

Where people actually live between the landmarks.

Maseru

A small capital on the Caledon, with the border post at the end of the main street.

Sandstone and Rondavel

Colonial sandstone in Maseru, and painted rondavels with stone walls in the mountains.

Sunday on Horseback

The horse is transport, not recreation, and every village has them saddled.

Ponies and Snow

Short pony treks, dinosaur footprints and, in winter, actual snow.

05

Adventure & outdoors

For travellers who would rather walk it than watch it.

Adventure

Pony Trekking

The Basotho pony is the way to cross this country, and has been for a hundred and fifty years.

Sani and Sehlabathebe

The Sani Pass in four-wheel-drive, and the rock formations and rivers of Sehlabathebe.

Lodges and Trading Posts

Malealea and Semonkong grew out of trading posts, and they are the best of it.

06

Travel inspiration

The frames people come home with.

High and Clear

The Maloti in autumn, when the grass turns and the air will not hold any haze.

Dinosaur Footprints

Trackways in the sandstone at Moyeni and Morija, in the open and unfenced.
