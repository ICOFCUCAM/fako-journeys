SENEGAL · Tourism

Where the Sahel Meets the Atlantic

The westernmost point of Africa, with Dakar on its peninsula, Gorée offshore, the Sine-Saloum delta, the Djoudj bird sanctuary and a music tradition that shaped a good deal of what came after.

West AfricaRegion

Where the Sahel meets the AtlanticKnown for
01

Featured destinations

Where the country actually takes you.

Delta and Dune

The Sine-Saloum mangrove delta, the Casamance forest, and Sahel sand in the north.

Fouta Djallon Foothills

No high peaks — the ground rises to the Fouta Djallon foothills in the far south-east.

Dindefelo

A hundred metres of water off the Fouta escarpment, near the Guinea border.

The Senegal and the Saloum

The Senegal river along the Mauritanian border, and the Saloum spreading into the sea.

The Petite Côte

Saly, Somone and Cap Skirring — Atlantic sand from the peninsula down to Casamance.

Casamance

Palm and rice country south of the Gambia, greener than the rest and quite different.

02

Wildlife & safari

What lives here, and how you see it without disturbing it.

Wildlife

Djoudj

Three million migrating birds winter in the Djoudj — one of the great bird sites on earth.

Niokolo-Koba

Lion, hippo and Derby eland in the south-east, in a park that is slowly recovering.

Community Campements

The Casamance campements villageois — village-owned, village-run, and thirty years old.

03

Culture & heritage

The part of a country you cannot photograph from a vehicle.

Culture

Teranga

The word means hospitality, and it is the thing the country most wants you to notice.

Wolof, Serer and Diola

Wolof across the country, Serer in the delta, and the Diola of Casamance.

Thieboudienne

Rice and fish, cooked in one pot, and the national dish by common consent.

Dak'Art and the Magal

The Dakar art biennale, and the Grand Magal of Touba, which moves millions of people.

Glass Painting and Weaving

Sous-verre glass painting, and the strip-woven cloth of the Manjak weavers.

Gorée

The island off Dakar, and the house of slaves that stands on it.

Saint-Louis

The old colonial capital on its island in the river mouth, with the Faidherbe bridge.

04

Cities & everyday life

Where people actually live between the landmarks.

Dakar

The most westerly city in Africa, on a peninsula, with the ocean on three sides.

Colonial and Sudano-Sahelian

The balconies of Saint-Louis, and the mud-and-timber mosques of the interior.

Ataya and the Market

Three glasses of tea, poured from height, and each one stronger than the last.

Short Flights, Long Beaches

Six hours from Europe, and beaches with a shallow warm Atlantic in front of them.

05

Adventure & outdoors

For travellers who would rather walk it than watch it.

Adventure

Surf, Sail, Paddle

Dakar's surf breaks, pirogues in the delta, and the Paris-Dakar's original finish line.

Surf and Sail

Dakar's breaks, kite-surfing on the Petite Côte, and pirogue trips through the mangrove.

Delta Lodges

Small lodges on the Saloum islands, reached only by boat, with birds outside.

06

Travel inspiration

The frames people come home with.

Light on Water

Delta light at either end of the day, and pink water at the Lac Rose.

Bassari Country

The Bassari and Bedik villages of the south-east, up in the hills, and rarely visited.
