01

Featured destinations

Where the country actually takes you.

The Sudd

One of the largest wetlands on earth, where the Nile spreads out and slows almost to a stop.

The Imatong

Mount Kinyeti at 3,187 metres on the Ugandan border, the highest point in the country.

Fula Falls

The Nile dropping through rock at Fula, on its way north out of Uganda.

The White Nile

It runs the length of the country and it is the reason for everything here.

No Coast, Plenty of Water

Landlocked, but the river islands and sandbars near Terekeka do the job.

The Southern Forests

Guinea-Congolian rainforest at the Congo border, with forest elephant and chimpanzee in it.

02

Wildlife & safari

What lives here, and how you see it without disturbing it.

Wildlife

The Great Kob Migration

White-eared kob, tiang and Mongalla gazelle — counted at over a million animals.

Boma and Badingilo

Two parks either side of the migration route, with almost no infrastructure and no crowds.

The Sudd Holds the Nile Back

Half the water entering the Sudd evaporates in it. The wetland is a climate system.

03

Culture & heritage

The part of a country you cannot photograph from a vehicle.

Culture

Sixty-Four Peoples

Dinka, Nuer, Shilluk, Bari, Azande, Mundari and fifty-eight more, in a country of twelve million.

The Mundari

Cattle are wealth, name and argument. A camp of them at dusk is unlike anything else.

Kisra, Asida and Fish

Sorghum asida, kisra flatbread, and Nile perch grilled on the bank.

Wrestling

Dinka and Mundari wrestling matches draw whole districts, with drumming and singing round the ring.

Beadwork and Corsets

Dinka beaded corsets in colours that state a man's age set, and Azande basketry.

Lado and the Old Posts

The Lado Enclave, Gondokoro and the river stations of the nineteenth-century Nile expeditions.

Nothing on the List Yet

No World Heritage sites, and a heritage that is living rather than ruined.

04

Cities & everyday life

Where people actually live between the landmarks.

Juba

A river port that became a capital in 2011 and has been building ever since.

Tukuls

Round mud walls, conical thatch, and a shape that has not needed improving.

Market Day at Terekeka

Cattle, sorghum, fish and tea, and the whole district in one place.

Hard Country for It

This is not a family destination yet, and pretending otherwise would not help anyone.

05

Adventure & outdoors

For travellers who would rather walk it than watch it.

Adventure

Getting to Boma

Light aircraft and then hours of track. It is expedition travel and it is honest about that.

Walking the Imatong

Cool forest, waterfalls and Kinyeti, from Gilo. Guides are essential and available.

Two Camps

There is a mobile camp on the migration and very little else. That is the honest position.

Smoke and Horns

A Mundari camp at first light, when the fires are lit and the light comes through them.

Nimule

A small park on the Ugandan border with elephant, and the Nile going through it.
