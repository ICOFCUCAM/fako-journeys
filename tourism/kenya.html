Where the Safari Was Invented

The Mara migration, Amboseli elephants under Kilimanjaro, the Rift Valley lakes, the Swahili coast and a capital with a national park on its doorstep.

East AfricaRegion

01

Featured destinations

Where the country actually takes you.

The Great Rift Valley

The escarpment drops away below the Naivasha road, holding a chain of soda lakes and dormant volcanoes.

Photo Sanjeed Quazi / Pexels

Mount Kenya, 5,199 m

Equatorial glaciers, giant lobelia, and a trekking route to Point Lenana that needs no technical climbing.

Photo Mary Rose Relente / Pexels

Thomson's Falls

The Ewaso Ng'iro drops seventy-four metres into a fern gorge at Nyahururu, on the way north to Samburu.

Photo Никита Шелайкин / Pexels

Flamingo Country

Nakuru, Bogoria and Elementaita hold soda water, hot springs and flamingo in numbers that change by the season.

Photo Kilinge Adventures / Pexels

The Swahili Coast

Diani, Watamu and Lamu: white sand, warm Indian Ocean, and a coastline that traded with Arabia for a thousand years.

Photo Zebari Visuals / Pexels

Kakamega, the Last of It

The only Kenyan remnant of the Guineo-Congolian rainforest that once ran clear across the continent.

Photo Ankit Bhattacharjee / Pexels

02

Wildlife & safari

What lives here, and how you see it without disturbing it.

Wildlife

Wildlife on the Savannah

The Big Five, plus the reticulated giraffe and Grevy's zebra that live nowhere south of here.

The Great Migration

Between July and October a million wildebeest cross the Mara river, and the crocodiles are already waiting.

Photo Hugo Sykes / Pexels

Rhino, Counted Daily

Ol Pejeta and Lewa run some of Africa's most closely monitored rhino populations, funded by the beds on the property.

Photo Twilight Kenya / Pexels

03

Culture & heritage

The part of a country you cannot photograph from a vehicle.

Culture

Maasai and Swahili

Two Kenyas: the cattle-keeping highlands and the coral-stone, Arabic-inflected coast.

Photo Mr Sketch / Pexels

Samburu and Maasai

Samburu in the northern drylands, Maasai in the south, both still moving cattle between seasonal grazing.

Photo josh_ dago__ / Pexels

Nyama Choma and Ugali

Goat roasted over charcoal, maize meal, sukuma wiki, and coastal biryani that came in by dhow.

Photo Justin Brian / Pexels

Lamu Cultural Festival

Dhow races, donkey races, henna and taarab music in the alleys of a town with almost no cars.

Photo Taryn Elliott / Pexels

Beadwork and Kisii Stone

Maasai beadwork, Kisii soapstone carving, and kiondo sisal baskets made the way they always were.

Photo Micah DeYoung / Pexels

Fort Jesus, Mombasa

Portuguese, built 1593, taken and retaken nine times — now UNESCO World Heritage above the old harbour.

Photo Thilina Alagiyawanna / Pexels

Lamu Old Town

The oldest continuously inhabited Swahili settlement, carved doors, coral rag walls and no room for a car.

Photo Keegan Checks / Pexels

04

Cities & everyday life

Where people actually live between the landmarks.

Nairobi, Green City in the Sun

The only capital on earth with a national park inside the city limits, and lions in sight of the skyline.

Photo Twilight Kenya / Pexels

Coral Stone and Carved Doors

Swahili coral-rag houses with inner courtyards, and doors carved in Lamu and shipped up the coast.

Photo ROMAN ODINTSOV / Pexels

Matatus and Markets

Painted, over-amplified matatus, Maasai Market on rotation through the week, and tea at every stop.

Photo Justin Brian / Pexels

The Conservancies

Community conservancies lease grazing land back for wildlife, and the lease pays the schools around the Mara.

Photo Lloyd Alozie / Pexels

05

Adventure & outdoors

For travellers who would rather walk it than watch it.

Adventure

Balloon Over the Mara

Up before dawn, an hour over the plains at treetop height, and breakfast on the grass where you land.

Photo Fali Poncha / Pexels

Walking Hell's Gate

The one Kenyan park you can walk and cycle through, with gorge scrambling and geothermal steam.

Photo Justin Brian / Pexels

Camps on the Mara

Small tented camps on private conservancies, night drives and off-road access the main reserve does not allow.

Photo Image Noise / Pexels

Travel inspiration

The frames people come home with.

Kilimanjaro from Amboseli

The mountain is in Tanzania; the view of it is in Kenya, and it is only clear for an hour at each end of the day.

Photo Ken Mwaura / Pexels

Chalbi and Marsabit

The desert north: Chalbi's salt pan, Marsabit's forested crater and the road down to Lake Turkana.

Photo Mary Rose Relente / Pexels
