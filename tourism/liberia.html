LIBERIA · Tourism

Sapo

Sapo National Park holds pygmy hippo and forest elephant in the second largest block of rainforest in West Africa, Robertsport has the best surf on the coast, and Monrovia was founded by freed American slaves in 1822.

01

Featured destinations

Where the country actually takes you.

The Upper Guinean Forest

Liberia holds about forty per cent of what is left of it.

Mount Nimba and Wologizi

Nimba on the Guinean border, and the Wologizi range with the highest ground in the country.

Kpatawee

Falls over granite ledges into a swimmable pool in Bong county.

The Saint Paul and the Cavalla

The Saint Paul runs past Monrovia; the Cavalla marks the whole Ivorian border.

Five Hundred Kilometres of Atlantic

Long empty beaches, and a serious swell from November on.

Sapo and Gola

Two blocks of high forest, both transboundary, and the best-protected in the region.

02

Wildlife & safari

What lives here, and how you see it without disturbing it.

Wildlife

The Pygmy Hippo

Nocturnal, solitary, forest-dwelling, and one of the rarest large mammals in Africa.

Forest Tracking

There is no game drive here. Sapo is walked, with trackers, over days.

Forty Per Cent of What Is Left

Liberia's forest is the largest surviving piece of the Upper Guinean block.

03

Culture & heritage

The part of a country you cannot photograph from a vehicle.

Culture

Americo-Liberian and Sixteen Others

A settler republic founded in 1822 over sixteen indigenous peoples, and the tension of that.

Kpelle, Bassa, Grebo, Vai

The Vai invented their own script in the 1830s, one of very few in Africa.

Jollof, Palm Butter and Fufu

Palm butter soup, cassava fufu, and a jollof Liberians will argue about at length.

Independence Day

26 July, and the parades, the food and the noise that come with it.

Masks and Country Cloth

Poro and Sande society masks in the interior, and woven country cloth everywhere.

Providence Island

Where the first settlers landed in 1822, in the middle of Monrovia's harbour.

A Republic Built by the Freed

Monrovia's nineteenth-century houses are the architecture of a return that did not go simply.

04

Cities & everyday life

Where people actually live between the landmarks.

Monrovia

A capital on a peninsula between the Atlantic and the Mesurado, rebuilt and rebuilding.

Verandah and Zinc

Americo-Liberian timber houses with deep verandahs, and zinc-roofed cityscape behind them.

The Beach at Sinkor

Monrovia goes to the beach bars along Sinkor at the weekend, and stays.

Kpatawee and the Coast

A waterfall pool inland and calm bays on the coast, both easy days out.

05

Adventure & outdoors

For travellers who would rather walk it than watch it.

Adventure

Surfing Robertsport

Cassava Point and Fisherman's Point — long left-hand point breaks, and a village surf club.

Sapo on Foot

Multi-day forest walking with rangers, sleeping in camps, and no vehicles at all.

Beginning

Beach lodges at Robertsport and a few good hotels in Monrovia.

Travel inspiration

The frames people come home with.

Green and Water

Sapo after rain, when the canopy drips and the light comes through in shafts.

Lake Piso

A lagoon behind Robertsport with mangrove, fishing villages and no visitors.
