01

Featured destinations

Where the country actually takes you.

The Albertine Rift

Glaciers on the Rwenzori, volcanoes in the Virungas, and the deepest forest in Africa between them.

The Rwenzori

5,109 metres at Margherita, glaciated, on the equator, and the Mountains of the Moon.

Boyoma and Lofoi

The seven cataracts at Kisangani, and Lofoi with a single drop of 340 metres.

The Congo River

Four and a half thousand kilometres, and the deepest river in the world at 220 metres.

Muanda, and the Lakes

Forty kilometres of Atlantic at the river mouth, and beaches on Kivu and Tanganyika.

Photo Abenezer Muluken / Pexels

The Ituri and the Salonga

Salonga is the largest tropical rainforest reserve in Africa, and mostly unentered.

Wildlife & safari

What lives here, and how you see it without disturbing it.

Wildlife

Mountain Gorillas and Okapi

Virunga's gorillas in the east, and the okapi in the Ituri forest, which lives nowhere else.

Garamba and Upemba

Northern savanna with elephant and Kordofan giraffe, being brought back after decades of loss.

Virunga's Rangers

Over two hundred rangers have died protecting this park. It is the oldest in Africa.

Culture & heritage

The part of a country you cannot photograph from a vehicle.

Culture

Two Hundred and Fifty Peoples

Four national languages, and a music that has set the terms for the whole continent.

Photo K / Pexels

The Mbuti

Forest hunter-gatherers of the Ituri, with a relationship to that forest going back millennia.

Photo Şeyhmus Kino / Pexels

Fufu, Pondu and Liboke

Cassava fufu, pondu cassava leaf, and fish steamed in leaves over a fire.

Photo Sue Hsu / Pexels

Rumba and Ndombolo

Franco, Papa Wemba, Koffi Olomide — Kinshasa has exported dance music for seventy years.

Masks, Raffia and Copper

Kuba raffia cloth, Pende and Songye masks, and Katangese copper crosses.

Kisangani and the River Stations

Stanley's route up the river, and the Wagenia fishing structures that predate all of it.

Five World Heritage Sites

Virunga, Garamba, Kahuzi-Biega, Salonga and the Okapi Reserve — all five on the danger list.

Photo Tuan Vy / Pexels
