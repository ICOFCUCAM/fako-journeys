Abidjan's lagoon skyline, the rainforest at Taï, the basilica at Yamoussoukro, and Atlantic beaches west towards Grand-Béréby — the economic engine of francophone West Africa.

01

Featured destinations

Where the country actually takes you.

Forest to Savanna

Rainforest in the south-west, a lagoon coast, and savanna running north to Mali.

Mount Nimba

The Nimba range on the Guinea and Liberia border, at 1,752 metres, with its own species.

Man and the Waterfalls

The falls around Man in the western hills, and the liana bridges strung above them.

The Ébrié and the Bandama

The lagoon system behind the coast, and the Bandama running the length of the country.

Assinie and Grand-Béréby

Assinie's sandbar east of Abidjan, and the quieter Atlantic beaches to the west.

Taï Rainforest

One of the last blocks of primary rainforest in West Africa, and chimpanzees that use tools.

02

Wildlife & safari

What lives here, and how you see it without disturbing it.

Wildlife

Taï and Comoé

Pygmy hippo and chimpanzees at Taï, and Comoé's savanna in the north-east.

Comoé

One of the largest protected areas in West Africa, with elephant, buffalo and hippo.

Taï and the Chimpanzees

Forty years of chimpanzee research at Taï, run with the villages around the park.

03

Culture & heritage

The part of a country you cannot photograph from a vehicle.

Culture

Sixty Peoples

Akan, Krou, Mandé and Gur — around sixty peoples inside one border.

Baoulé, Senufo and Dan

Baoulé weaving, Senufo sculpture, and Dan masks from the western hills.

Attiéké and Kedjenou

Attiéké with grilled fish, and kedjenou slow-cooked in a sealed pot.

Abissa and the Masks

Abissa at Grand-Bassam in November, and the mask festivals of the western hills.

Weaving and Carving

Baoulé strip cloth, Korhogo painted cloth, and Dan masks carved for use rather than sale.

Grand-Bassam

The first colonial capital, on a sandbar between lagoon and ocean, and now a world site.

Yamoussoukro

A basilica larger than Saint Peter's, built in the founder's home village.

04

Cities & everyday life

Where people actually live between the landmarks.

Abidjan

The Plateau's towers, the Ébrié lagoon, and a nightlife the region measures itself against.

Lagoon Colonial

The wooden trading houses of Grand-Bassam, and Abidjan's tropical modernism.

Maquis at Night

The open-air maquis — grill, music and a plastic chair — is the country's real institution.

Beach and Lagoon

Assinie's sandbar has ocean on one side and calm lagoon on the other, which suits children.

05

Adventure & outdoors

For travellers who would rather walk it than watch it.

Adventure

Surf, Hike, Paddle

Surf at Assinie, hiking the western hills, and pirogues through the lagoon.

Surf and Hike

Assinie's breaks, and the liana bridges and falls in the hills around Man.

Assinie and Abidjan

Lagoon villas at Assinie, and Abidjan hotels that serve the whole region's business.

Pirogues on flat water at either end of the day, and a skyline behind them.

Man
