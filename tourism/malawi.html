MALAWI · Tourism

The Lake

Lake Malawi holds more freshwater fish species than any lake on earth, Liwonde and Majete have been restocked to the point of holding all the big five, and the Mulanje massif rises three thousand metres straight off the plain.

Freshwater Beaches

Culture & heritage

Culture

The Warm Heart

The reputation for friendliness is real, and it is the first thing every visitor mentions.

Photo Feyza Daştan / Pexels

Chewa, Yao, Tumbuka, Lomwe

The Chewa Gule Wamkulu masquerade is on the UNESCO list and is danced still.

Photo Mad Knoxx Deluxe / Pexels

Nsima and Chambo

Nsima with relish, and chambo — a lake cichlid — grilled whole on the shore.

Photo Alexis Ricardo Alaurin / Pexels

The Lake of Stars

A music festival on the shore, and Gule Wamkulu in the villages all year.

Photo Ravi Rajapaksha / Pexels

Carving and Chitenje

Blackwood carving, basketry, and the printed chitenje cloth worn everywhere.

Photo Raphhill .Jpg / Pexels

Livingstonia

A mission on the escarpment built in 1894, with the manse, the church and the view intact.

Lake Malawi National Park

The first freshwater national park in the world, listed for its cichlids.
