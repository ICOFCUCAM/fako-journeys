CABO VERDE · Tourism

Fogo

Ten Atlantic islands six hundred kilometres off Senegal: Fogo is an active volcano with vineyards inside the caldera, Santo Antao is cut into ribeiras you walk down, and the morna that Cesaria Evora sang is on the UNESCO list.

01

Featured destinations

Where the country actually takes you.

Ten Islands, Ten Climates

Desert dunes on Boa Vista and cloud forest on Santo Antao, in the same archipelago.

Pico do Fogo and Santo Antao

Fogo's cone, and Santo Antao's ridges where the paths run along the crest.

Seasonal Only

The rain comes in August and September, and the ribeiras run for a few weeks.

Ribeiras

Steep valleys terraced for sugarcane and coffee, and irrigated by hand-cut channels.

Sal, Boa Vista and Tarrafal

White sand and constant wind on Sal and Boa Vista, and quiet bays on Santiago.

The Cloud Forest of Santo Antao

The north of Santo Antao catches enough cloud to grow coffee and dragon trees.

02

Wildlife & safari

What lives here, and how you see it without disturbing it.

Wildlife

Turtles and Humpbacks

Boa Vista is one of the largest loggerhead nesting sites in the world.

Sea, Not Savanna

No large land animals. What there is instead is turtles, whales and seabirds.

Turtle Patrols on Boa Vista

Community and NGO patrols on the nesting beaches, and the numbers have recovered.

03

Culture & heritage

The part of a country you cannot photograph from a vehicle.

Culture

Creole, and the Morna

A Portuguese-African creole culture, and a music that put these islands on the map.

The Badiu and the Sampadjudu

The Santiago interior and the northern islands, with different histories and different music.

Cachupa and Grogue

Cachupa — the slow-cooked maize and bean stew — and grogue distilled from cane.

Mindelo Carnival and Baia das Gatas

Carnival in February and a music festival on the beach in August.

Pottery, Panu and Grogue

The panu woven cloth of Santiago, Fogo's pottery, and cane spirit stills.

Cidade Velha

The first European colonial town in the tropics, founded in 1462, with the fort above it.

Cidade Velha, Listed

Inscribed in 2009 — the pillory, the cathedral ruins and the street plan of the first town.

04

Cities & everyday life

Where people actually live between the landmarks.

Praia and Mindelo

A clifftop capital on Santiago, and the port city on Sao Vicente where the music lives.

Portuguese Colonial and Volcanic Stone

Pastel colonial in Mindelo, and black volcanic stone houses on Fogo.

The Bar at Midnight

Mindelo's bars start late and the morna does not begin until they are full.

Beaches and Short Flights

Calm water on Sal and Boa Vista, short hops between islands, and a very easy pace.

05

Adventure & outdoors

For travellers who would rather walk it than watch it.

Adventure

Kitesurfing and Ridge Walking

Sal and Boa Vista for wind, and Santo Antao for some of the best walking in the Atlantic.

Walking Santo Antao

Cobbled paths from the ridge down to the sea, a day at a time, between villages.

Well Established

Resorts on Sal and Boa Vista, and small good hotels on Santo Antao and Fogo.
