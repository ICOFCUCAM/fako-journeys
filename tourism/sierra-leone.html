The Freetown Peninsula

A peninsula where rainforest mountains come down to white sand, Tiwai island with eleven primate species, and the Banana and Turtle Islands offshore — the best beaches in West Africa with almost nobody on them.

01

Featured destinations

Where the country actually takes you.

The Loma Mountains

Cloud forest and montane grassland in the northeast, hardly walked at all.

Bintumani

1,945 metres in the Loma range, the highest point in West Africa west of Cameroon.

Charlotte and Bumbuna

Charlotte falls in the peninsula forest, and Bumbuna on the Rokel in the interior.

The Moa and the Rokel

The Moa runs past Tiwai, and the Rokel comes down to the estuary at Freetown.

River No. 2 and the Banana Islands

White sand, forest behind, and two small islands an hour offshore with reef around them.

Gola Rainforest

The largest remaining block of Upper Guinean forest, now a transboundary park with Liberia.

Wildlife & safari

What lives here, and how you see it without disturbing it.

Wildlife

Eleven Primates at Tiwai

An island in the Moa river with one of the highest primate densities anywhere.

Forest, Not Plain

Pygmy hippo, forest elephant and bongo in Gola — a forest circuit, not a savanna one.

Tiwai, Community-Run

Eight villages own the island reserve and have done since before the war.

03

Culture & heritage

The part of a country you cannot photograph from a vehicle.

Culture

Krio, and Sixteen Others

Freetown was founded by freed slaves, and Krio is the language and the culture that came of it.

Mende, Temne and Krio

Mende in the south, Temne in the north, and the Krio of the peninsula.

Cassava Leaf and Country Rice

Cassava leaf with palm oil, groundnut stew, and fish straight off the beach.

Lantern Parade

Illuminated floats through Freetown on the night of Independence Day.

Country Cloth and Gara

Hand-woven country cloth strips, and gara tie-dye in indigo and kola.

Bunce Island

A slaving fort in the estuary, ruined and overgrown, and among the most affecting in Africa.

The Cotton Tree, and What Followed

Freetown grew around one tree where freed slaves gave thanks in 1792.

04

Cities & everyday life

Where people actually live between the landmarks.

Freetown

Krio board houses on the hills, the estuary below, and mountains directly behind.

Krio Board Houses

Timber-framed, shuttered, verandahed — an architecture that came back across the Atlantic.

Sunday at Lumley

The whole city goes to the beach, and the bars along it play until late.

Beaches With Shade

River No. 2 and Tokeh have forest behind the sand and calm water in the dry season.

Adventure

Surfing and Forest Walking

Bureh has a surf club run by the village, and Tiwai has night walks for pottos.

Climbing Bintumani

Four days from Sinekoro, through forest and out onto grassland above the cloud.

Growing

Good beach lodges on the peninsula, and Tiwai's community camp inland.
