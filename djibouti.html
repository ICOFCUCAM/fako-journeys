Where three plates pull apart.

Landscapes
  • The Afar TriangleThree tectonic plates pulling apart, and you can stand in the crack.
  • The Goda and MablaMousa Ali at 2,028 metres, and juniper forest above the coastal plain at Day.
  • Nothing PermanentThere are no rivers here. The water is in wadis, springs and the sea.
  • Lake AbbeLimestone chimneys steaming on a dry lake bed, on the Ethiopian border.
  • The Gulf of TadjouraBeaches at Sable Blanc and the Sept Freres islands, and reef straight off the sand.
  • The Last JuniperThe Day forest is a relic of a wetter Horn, and it is shrinking.
Wildlife
  • Whale SharksThey come into the Gulf of Tadjoura from November, in numbers, close to shore.
  • Day ForestBeira antelope and the last Djibouti francolin in the juniper of the Goda mountains.
  • Salt, Cut by HandSlabs still cut from Assal and loaded onto camels, on a route a thousand years old.
  • The ArdoukobaA volcano that erupted in 1978 as the rift opened. The lava field is still bare.
People and culture
  • Afar and SomaliTwo peoples, French and Arabic official, and a port that half the Horn depends on.
  • The AfarSalt caravans still come up from Assal, and the Afar have worked that trade for centuries.
  • Independence Day27 June, and the Afar and Somali dance traditions that come out for it.
  • Weaving and WoodworkAfar mats and milk vessels, and carved wooden headrests that double as stools.
  • Skoudehkaris and Yemeni FishSpiced rice with lamb, and whole fish baked in a Yemeni clay oven on the coast.
  • Sable BlancShallow, warm, empty white sand an hour and a half from the capital.
  • Khat at FourThe flight lands from Ethiopia and the city stops for it. Every afternoon.
Places
  • Djibouti CityA French-Arab port city with a covered market, and the Horn's busiest container terminal.
  • Arcades and CoralColonial arcades in the European quarter, and coral-block houses in the old town.
  • Ottoman TadjouraOne of the oldest towns on this coast, with seven mosques and a sultan's history.
  • The Lake Abbe LandscapeNot listed, but the chimneys are on every list of the strangest places on earth.
Ways to travel
  • Snorkelling and Rift WalkingSwimming with whale sharks in the morning, walking a spreading rift in the afternoon.
  • Small and Getting BetterA few good hotels in the capital and a diving lodge on the gulf.
  • White on TurquoiseLake Assal at midday, when the salt is blinding and the water is the wrong blue.
  • The Sept FreresSeven islands at the mouth of the gulf, with the best reef in the country round them.
