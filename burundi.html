Landscapes
  • The Congo-Nile DivideA ridge running the length of the country, with the Nile on one side and the Congo on the other.
  • Mount Heha2,684 metres, an hour from the capital, with the whole rift visible from the top.
  • KareraFour falls in one forested gorge in the southeast, with a path between them.
  • Lake TanganyikaThe second deepest lake on earth, and Burundi has a hundred and sixty kilometres of it.
  • Saga and Karera BeachesWhite sand on Tanganyika south of Bujumbura, with the Congo visible across the water.
  • KibiraForty thousand hectares of montane forest on the divide, and the country's water tower.
Wildlife
  • Chimpanzees at KibiraMontane forest continuous with Rwanda's Nyungwe, with chimpanzee and colobus in it.
  • Rusizi and RuvubuHippo and crocodile in the Rusizi delta, and buffalo and antelope along the Ruvubu.
  • Kibira's WaterThe forest feeds the hydro that runs most of the country, and protecting it is not sentiment.
  • Hiking the HillsHeha, Kibira and the ridge roads, none of them more than a day.
People and culture
  • The DrumsThe royal drummers of Gishora are on the UNESCO list, and they are not a performance troupe.
  • Hutu, Tutsi and TwaOne language, Kirundi, spoken by everybody, and a history the country is still working through.
  • GishoraThe drum sanctuary above Gitega, where the drums are kept and danced on their own ground.
  • Basketry and DrumsCoiled agaseke baskets with pointed lids, and the drums themselves.
  • Beans, Ugali and MukekeBeans and plantain, ugali, and mukeke — a Tanganyika fish grilled on the beach.
  • Lake and HillsShort distances, calm fresh water, and hippos to keep an eye on.
  • The Beach on a SundayBujumbura goes to Saga beach, and stays until the light goes.
Places
  • Bujumbura and GitegaA lakeside city with a long promenade, and the political capital up in the hills.
  • Colonial and HillsideGerman and Belgian colonial in Bujumbura, and tiled hillside houses everywhere else.
  • RutovuA stone pyramid on a ridge marking the southernmost source of the Nile.
  • The Ritual Dance of the Royal DrumInscribed in 2014 — the drums, the dance and the making of them, together.
Ways to travel
  • Walking the DivideKibira on foot, Mount Heha in a morning, and the lake shore road south.
  • ModestA handful of hotels in Bujumbura, and lodges on the lake shore.
  • Drums in MotionGishora mid-performance, when the drums are carried and struck at once.
  • Rusizi DeltaHippos, birds and the Congo across the water, twenty minutes from the capital.
