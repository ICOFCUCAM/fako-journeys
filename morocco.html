01Highlights

What Morocco is known for.

Six of the twenty-seven, and the ones most people come for. The rest are below — and none of them is filler.

Nature & Landscapes

The Draa and the Dades

Palm oases strung along the Draa, and gorges cut red through the Dades and the Todra.

Wildlife

Barbary Macaques and the Argan Forest

Macaques in the cedar forest above Azrou, and goats standing in the argan trees near Essaouira.

Mountains

The High Atlas

Toubkal is 4,167 metres and the highest point in North Africa, walkable in two days from Imlil.

Beaches & Coast

The Atlantic Coast

Essaouira's wind, Taghazout's point breaks, and the long empty beaches between them.

Forests & Rainforest

The Cedar Forest

The Middle Atlas cedar, some of it a thousand years old, above Ifrane and Azrou.

Culture

Amazigh and Arab

Three Amazigh languages, Arabic, French and Spanish, and a culture that predates all of them.

Two Days, Four Climates

The blue town in the Rif, and a mountain range most itineraries drive straight past.

Eco Tourism

Oasis Agriculture

The khettara irrigation channels still working, and the argan cooperatives run by women.

Heritage

The Imperial Cities

Fez, Marrakesh, Meknes and Rabat — four capitals, each of a different dynasty.
