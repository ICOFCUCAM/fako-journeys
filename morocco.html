Landscapes
  • The Draa and the DadesPalm oases strung along the Draa, and gorges cut red through the Dades and the Todra.
  • The High AtlasToubkal is 4,167 metres and the highest point in North Africa, walkable in two days from Imlil.
  • OuzoudA hundred metres of water in three stages, with macaques in the olive trees beside it.
  • The Draa RiverThe longest river in Morocco, mostly dry, and green the whole way where it is not.
  • The Atlantic CoastEssaouira's wind, Taghazout's point breaks, and the long empty beaches between them.
  • The Cedar ForestThe Middle Atlas cedar, some of it a thousand years old, above Ifrane and Azrou.
Wildlife
  • Barbary Macaques and the Argan ForestMacaques in the cedar forest above Azrou, and goats standing in the argan trees near Essaouira.
  • Desert CountryNot the safari of the east — the wildlife here is desert-adapted, and you look for it at first light.
  • Oasis AgricultureThe khettara irrigation channels still working, and the argan cooperatives run by women.
  • Trek, Surf, RideMulti-day Atlas treks, Atlantic surf schools, and horses on the Essaouira sand.
People and culture
  • Amazigh and ArabThree Amazigh languages, Arabic, French and Spanish, and a culture that predates all of them.
  • The People of the AtlasVillage life at two thousand metres, where the year is still organised around the snow.
  • Moussem and GnaouaThe Gnaoua festival in Essaouira in June, and the moussems that mark the harvest.
  • Zellige, Leather and WoolTile cut by hand in Fez, tanned leather, and carpets woven to patterns held in memory.
  • Tagine, Bread and MintSlow-cooked tagine, bread baked in the neighbourhood oven, and tea poured from height.
  • Easy TravellingShort drives, good roads, and a train line down the coast that children enjoy.
  • The Souk in the MorningBefore the tour groups, the medina is simply where people buy their vegetables.
Places
  • Marrakesh and CasablancaJemaa el-Fnaa at dusk, and the Atlantic modernism of Casablanca an hour up the road.
  • Riad and MedinaRooms built inward around a courtyard, because the street was never meant to see in.
  • Volubilis and the KasbahsA Roman city on the plain at Volubilis, and Ait Benhaddou standing above the Ounila.
  • The Imperial CitiesFez, Marrakesh, Meknes and Rabat — four capitals, each of a different dynasty.
Ways to travel
  • Dunes, Gorges and SurfTrekking in the Atlas, surfing the Atlantic points, and a night out on the erg at Chegaga.
  • Desert Camps and RiadsErg Chigaga camps well beyond the day-trip dunes, and riads restored room by room.
  • Light on Earth WallsPisé takes late light the way nothing else does, which is why every photographer comes here.
  • Chefchaouen and the RifThe blue town in the Rif, and a mountain range most itineraries drive straight past.
