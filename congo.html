Landscapes
  • The Congo BasinTwo thirds of the country is rainforest, and much of it has never had a road in it.
  • Mount Berongou903 metres in the Mayombe, and the highest ground in a country of forest and plateau.
  • The LoufoulakariFalls on the Congo above Brazzaville, where the river drops through rock.
  • The Congo RiverThe second largest river on earth by volume, and the whole eastern border of the country.
  • A Hundred and Seventy Kilometres of AtlanticPointe-Noire's beaches, and the empty sand of the Conkouati coast north of it.
  • Nouabale-NdokiForest that has never been logged, and the Goualougo Triangle where the chimps had not seen people.
Wildlife
  • Western Lowland GorillasSmaller, redder and harder to see than mountain gorillas, and tracked through swamp forest.
  • Bai WatchingForest buffalo, bongo, sitatunga and elephant come to the clearings, and you sit and wait.
  • Odzala's ModelEx-poachers employed as trackers, and revenue shared with the villages on the park edge.
  • Tracking and PirogueLong days on foot through swamp forest, and quiet mornings on the Lekoli.
People and culture
  • Congolese RumbaRumba was made on both banks of this river, and it is on the UNESCO list because of it.
  • Kongo, Teke, Mbochi and the AkaBantu peoples along the river, and Aka forest hunter-gatherers in the north.
  • The Feux de BrazzaA music festival in Brazzaville, and rumba the rest of the year in every bar.
  • Raffia, Masks and NkisiRaffia weaving, Teke masks, and Kongo nkisi power figures.
  • Saka-Saka and Poulet MoambeCassava leaf, and chicken in palm nut sauce — the dish of both Congos.
  • Not ReallyOdzala is a long way in and expensive. This is a trip for adults who want gorillas.
  • The Bars of Poto-PotoRumba, grilled fish and beer, from the afternoon until it is not the afternoon.
Places
  • Brazzaville and Pointe-NoireA river capital looking across at Kinshasa, and an Atlantic oil port with the beaches.
  • Colonial and ForestFrench colonial along the river, and the Poto-Poto quarter that gave its name to a painting school.
  • Brazzaville's Colonial CoreThe Case de Gaulle and the Basilique Sainte-Anne, and a river frontage facing Kinshasa.
  • Sangha TrinationalNouabale-Ndoki is part of the World Heritage forest shared with Cameroon and CAR.
Ways to travel
  • Forest and RiverPirogue on the Lekoli, tracking on foot, and nights on a bai platform.
  • Small and ExcellentOdzala's camps are among the best-run in Central Africa, and there are three of them.
  • Green and SteamA bai at first light, when the mist is still on it and the elephants are already there.
  • Lesio-LounaA gorilla sanctuary on the Bateke plateau, two hours from Brazzaville.
