01Highlights

What Zimbabwe is known for.

Six of the twenty-seven, and the ones most people come for. The rest are below — and none of them is filler.

Nature & Landscapes

Highveld and Escarpment

Granite kopjes across the highveld, and the Zambezi escarpment dropping into the valley.

Wildlife

Hwange's Elephant

Forty thousand elephant in Hwange, drinking at pumped pans through the dry season.

Mountains

The Eastern Highlands

Nyanga, Vumba and Chimanimani along the Mozambique border, up to 2,592 metres.

Beaches & Coast

Landlocked

No coast — the water here is the Zambezi, Kariba, and the pools of the Mana floodplain.

Forests & Rainforest

Msasa and Miombo

Miombo woodland across the country, and msasa that comes into red leaf in September.

Culture

Shona and Ndebele

Shona across the centre and east, Ndebele in the west, and stone sculpture from both.
