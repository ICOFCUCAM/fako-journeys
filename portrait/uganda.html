Ours, not theirs

How to be here

The one thing on this page that is a position rather than a fact. It is not any country’s rule and no community here was asked to write it — it is what Afrinkong asks of the people it sends, and the reasons are the part worth carrying.

With a camera

Ask before you photograph a person A face is not scenery, and 'they did not seem to mind' is not the same as being asked — if you would not want the picture taken of you at work, do not take it.

If a photograph is worth money, pay it Somebody who poses for a living is working; somebody who does not should not be turned into a subject because a lens arrived.

Some places are not for cameras Shrines, funerals, initiations and some interiors are closed to photography by the people who use them, and a guide will tell you which if you ask before rather than after.

Children are not content Ask the adult responsible for them, the way you would at home, and accept no as an answer the first time.

With people

Go with somebody from here The three operators on this site are companies registered in the countries they work in — which is the difference between money that lands and money that passes through. Nineteen countries here have no operator of ours yet, and asking who is running a day is a fair question to put to whoever sells it to you.

Learn the greeting Two words in the language of the place you are standing in changes the whole shape of a conversation, and getting it slightly wrong is not the insult you are afraid of.

Ask what a thing is A festival, a garment or a building usually means something specific to a specific community, and the interesting answer is never the one you brought with you.

With the place

Keep the distance you are given The distances on a gorilla trek or at a nest are set by the animals' tolerance and, in the case of primates, by what they can catch from you.

Do not feed anything An animal that associates people with food stops being wild and starts being a problem to be dealt with, usually permanently.

Carry out what you carried in Most of the places on this site sit outside any collection route, which means what you leave stays exactly where you left it.

With money

Buy from the maker The craft write-ups on this site name workshops and markets; a piece bought where it was made pays the person who made it rather than three people who did not.

Haggle to a fair price, not to a win The last few units of currency are worth more to the seller than to you, and a bargain that felt like a victory usually was not one.
