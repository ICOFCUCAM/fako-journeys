Nigeria

The loudest country on the continent.

One in five Africans is Nigerian. Afrobeats, Nollywood, the Benin bronzes, Yankari's elephants and the Obudu plateau — a country that exports its culture faster than anywhere else on earth.

01Highlights

What Nigeria is known for.

Six of the twenty-seven, and the ones most people come for. The rest are below — and none of them is filler.

Nature & Landscapes

Mangrove to Sahel

Niger delta mangrove in the south, rainforest, savanna, and the Sahel at the Niger border.

Wildlife

Yankari and Gashaka

Yankari's elephant herds, and Gashaka-Gumti's forest chimpanzees on the Cameroon border.

Mountains

The Obudu Plateau

Sixteen hundred metres of grassland on the Cameroon border, with cattle ranches and cloud.

Beaches & Coast

Lagos and the Atlantic

Tarkwa Bay, Elegushi and the long Atlantic beaches east towards Lekki.

Forests & Rainforest

Cross River

The Cross River forest holds the last of Nigeria's gorillas and some of West Africa's tallest trees.

Culture

Five Hundred Languages

Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa and around five hundred more, in one country.
