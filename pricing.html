03In and out

What the rate covers, and what it plainly does not.

We would rather lose a booking on an honest list than win one and argue about it in Maroua. If something you expected is not on the left-hand column, ask before you pay the deposit.

Included in every circuit rate
  • Licensed guide throughout, working in French and English
  • Vehicle and driver — Land Cruiser or Hilux, fuel, tolls and parking
  • All park permits and community levies listed above
  • Accommodation on the stated basis, twin share
  • Breakfast daily, plus all meals on trekking and camping days
  • Porters and camp crew where the circuit requires them
  • Drinking water in the vehicle and on the hill, 3 litres per person per day
  • Airport transfers at Douala, Garoua or Maroua on circuit days
Not included — budget for these separately
  • International flights and the Cameroon visa
  • Internal flights Douala–Garoua, quoted live at time of booking
  • Travel insurance, which is a condition of booking with us
  • Lunches and dinners on road and city days, roughly 8 000 FCFA a head
  • Drinks other than the water we carry
  • Single supplement, from 22 000 FCFA per night where a single room exists
  • Tips for guides, porters and drivers — customary, never compulsory
  • Yellow fever certificate and antimalarials, both genuinely required
04Deposits & terms

How a booking is held, paid and unwound if it has to be.

The same terms apply to every circuit and to every traveller, whether you book direct or through an agent. They are repeated in full on the confirmation we email you before any money moves.

What confirms a booking?

Twenty-five per cent of the circuit total, plus the full cost of any internal flight we hold for you. The date, the vehicle and the guides are blocked the moment it clears.

When is the balance due?

Forty-five days before departure. Inside forty-five days a booking is payable in full at the time it is made.

How can we pay?

Bank transfer to our Douala account in FCFA or euro, MTN MoMo and Orange Money for regional travellers, or a secure card link that carries a 3.5 per cent processor fee.

What if we cancel?

Over 60 days, deposit held as credit for eighteen months. 60 to 30 days, 40 per cent of the total. Under 30 days, 75 per cent. Under 7 days, no refund — the crew and rooms are paid by then.

What if you cancel?

If we stand a circuit down for weather, security guidance or our own operational failure, you choose between a full refund and a rebooking at the price you originally paid.

Are there reductions?

Children under twelve travel at 70 per cent on grade 1 and 2 circuits. Cameroonian and CEMAC residents pay the resident park tariff, which we pass straight through.
