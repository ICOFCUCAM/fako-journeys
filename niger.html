Niger

The Air, the Tenere, and the last giraffes of West Africa.

The Tenere is the emptiest desert on earth, the Air massif rises out of it to two thousand metres, and the last West African giraffes live wild an hour from the capital.

25 ways to spend the time.

Landscapes
  • The Air MassifGranite mountains standing two thousand metres out of the sand, with guelta pools in them.
  • Mont Idoukal-n-Taghes2,022 metres in the Air, and the high point of a range full of rock art.
  • Guelta PoolsPermanent rock pools in the Air, the only standing water for hundreds of kilometres.
  • The Niger at NiameyThe river runs 550 kilometres across the southwest and the whole country leans on it.
  • Sandbars on the RiverLandlocked, and the swimming is off the sandbanks at Niamey when the river is low.
  • The Doum Palm ValleysPalm-filled wadis in the Air, green lines through absolutely bare rock.
Wildlife
  • The Last West African GiraffeAbout six hundred of them at Kouré, the only wild population left in the whole of West Africa.
  • W National ParkShared with Benin and Burkina Faso — lion, elephant and buffalo on the Niger river.
  • Giraffes Beside FarmsKouré's giraffes live among fields, and the community is paid to keep it that way.
  • The Air on Foot and CamelWeeks of walking or riding between guelta and village, with Tuareg guides.
People and culture
  • Tuareg, Hausa, Zarma, FulaniTuareg and Toubou in the north, Hausa and Zarma in the south, Wodaabe in between.
  • The WodaabeThe Gerewol, where the men dress and dance and the women choose.
  • The Cure SaleeTuareg and Wodaabe gather at In-Gall in September when the salt pastures come up.
  • Tuareg Silver and LeatherThe Agadez cross in silver, and camel saddles and boxes in tooled leather.
  • Millet, Djerma Rice and KilishiMillet paste with sauce, and kilishi — beef dried thin and spiced, the best in the region.
  • Giraffes an Hour AwayKouré is a morning trip from Niamey, on foot, and children can do it.
  • The Niamey Grand MarcheEverything from millet to motorcycle parts, and the noise that goes with it.
Places
  • Niamey and AgadezA river capital with the best museum in the Sahel, and a caravan city at the desert edge.
  • Mud and BeamsSahelian mud with projecting timber, and the Agadez minaret as its tallest expression.
  • AgadezA mud minaret twenty-seven metres high, and the Sultanate that has stood behind it since 1449.
  • Three SitesAgadez, the Air and Tenere reserves, and W National Park — desert, mountain and river.
Ways to travel
  • Crossing the TenereThe Tree of Tenere marked the crossing until a truck hit it in 1973. The route is still run.
  • Very LittleA few hotels in Niamey. Everything north is camps and hospitality.
  • Dunes and NothingThe Tenere at either end of the day, where the only vertical thing is your own shadow.
  • The Bilma Salt CaravansThe azalai still runs from Agadez to Bilma and back, a thousand kilometres by camel.
