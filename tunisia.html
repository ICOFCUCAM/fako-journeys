Landscapes
  • Coast to ChottThe Cap Bon coast, the olive Sahel, and the salt flats of the Chott el Djerid.
  • The DorsaleThe Atlas tail crosses the north — Jebel Chambi is 1,544 metres and the highest point.
  • The Oasis CascadesChebika, Tamerza and Mides — the mountain oases of the west, with water in a dry country.
  • The MedjerdaThe only permanent river, running out of Algeria and across the northern plain.
  • The MediterraneanHammamet, Djerba and Cap Bon — twelve hundred kilometres of it, and warm from May.
  • Cork Oak in the NorthThe Kroumirie forest of cork oak in the north-west, which surprises everyone.
Wildlife
  • Ichkeul and the DesertIchkeul's wintering waterfowl, and the desert fox and gazelle of the south.
  • Not a Safari CountryThe wildlife here is birds and desert species, and Ichkeul is the reason to bring binoculars.
  • Oasis WaterThe oasis irrigation of Tozeur and Nefta, managing water in the driest place in the country.
  • Desert and DiveDune trekking from Douz, quad routes on the chott, and diving at Tabarka.
People and culture
  • Punic, Roman, Arab, BerberFour layers, all of them still visible, and often in the same town.
  • The Berber SouthThe ksour of Tataouine, and the underground houses of Matmata.
  • Carthage and the Sahara FestivalThe Carthage film and music festival in summer, and the Douz Sahara festival in December.
  • Mosaic, Ceramic and SilverRoman mosaic technique still taught, Nabeul ceramic, and Berber silver from the south.
  • Couscous, Brik and HarissaFriday couscous, brik straight out of the pan, and harissa on everything.
  • Two Hours from EuropeShort flights, shallow warm sea, and resorts that have been doing families for decades.
  • The Souk and the CaféMint tea with pine nuts, and a souk that still mostly sells to the people who live there.
Places
  • Tunis and SfaxTunis at the head of its lake, and Sfax's walled medina working as a medina still.
  • Whitewash and BlueSidi Bou Said's blue and white, and the vaulted ksour of the desert south.
  • Carthage, Dougga, El JemEl Jem's amphitheatre seats thirty-five thousand and is standing almost complete.
  • The MedinasTunis, Sousse and Kairouan — Kairouan's mosque is among the oldest in the Maghreb.
Ways to travel
  • Desert, Diving, DunesCamel treks from Douz, dune camps on the erg, and diving off Tabarka.
  • Riad, Dar and Dune CampRestored dar houses in the medinas, and desert camps beyond the last palm.
  • Light on WhiteWhitewash, blue, and desert light — the reason the film crews keep coming back.
  • Ksar Ouled SoltaneVaulted grain stores stacked four storeys high, and almost nobody there.
