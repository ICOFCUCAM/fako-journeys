Landscapes
  • Reef and MiomboCoral reef the whole coast, miombo woodland inland, and the Rift's southern end at Gorongosa.
  • Mount Namuli and GorongosaNamuli at 2,419 metres, and Gorongosa's own mountain with rainforest on top of it.
  • Murchison of the SouthWaterfalls off the Zambezi escarpment, and the Lugenda's rapids in the far north.
  • The Zambezi and Cahora BassaThe Zambezi's last five hundred kilometres, and the Cahora Bassa lake above them.
  • Bazaruto and the QuirimbasDune islands in the south, coral islands in the north, and dhows between them.
  • Coastal ForestThe coastal dry forest of the north, and the mangrove that lines most of the delta.
Wildlife
  • GorongosaA park that lost almost everything in the war and has spent twenty years getting it back.
  • Niassa and GorongosaNiassa is one of the largest reserves in Africa, and among the least visited anywhere.
  • Gorongosa's RestorationLocal employment, local science, and a park brought back from nothing — worth understanding.
  • Dive and SailWhale shark season at Tofo, dhow safaris in the Quirimbas, and reef diving throughout.
People and culture
  • Portuguese and SwahiliFive hundred years of Portuguese on a Swahili coast, and a culture made from both.
  • Makonde and MakuaMakonde carving in the north, and the Makua women's mussiro face paint.
  • Marrabenta and TimbilaMarrabenta on the coast, and the Chopi timbila orchestras of Inhambane.
  • Carving and BasketryMakonde blackwood carving, and the palm basketry of the northern islands.
  • Peri-Peri and MatapaPrawns with peri-peri, and matapa — cassava leaf and coconut — with rice.
  • Warm Water All YearShallow, warm and calm inside the reef — as easy a sea as the continent offers.
  • The Beach MarketFish landed and sold on the sand, and the dhow that brought it still on the beach.
Places
  • MaputoWide avenues, a Mediterranean seafront, and the market Eiffel's company built.
  • Coral Stone and ColonialCoral rag on the islands, and Portuguese modernism through Maputo and Beira.
  • Ilha de MoçambiqueThe old capital, on a coral island a kilometre long, with a fort at one end.
  • The Swahili CoastIbo, Angoche and Sofala — Indian Ocean trading towns older than the Portuguese.
Ways to travel
  • Dive, Sail, KayakManta and whale shark at Tofo, dhow safaris in the Quirimbas, and kayaks in the mangrove.
  • Island LodgesA handful of very good island lodges in Bazaruto and the Quirimbas, and little else on them.
  • Dhow LightLateen sails at either end of the day, and water that photographs as three colours at once.
  • Ibo IslandSilversmiths, a fort, mangrove channels, and about as far from a resort as it gets.
