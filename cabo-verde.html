Cabo Verde

Ten islands, and the morna.

Ten Atlantic islands six hundred kilometres off Senegal: Fogo is an active volcano with vineyards inside the caldera, Santo Antao is cut into ribeiras you walk down, and the morna that Cesaria Evora sang is on the UNESCO list.

25 ways to spend the time.

Landscapes
  • Ten Islands, Ten ClimatesDesert dunes on Boa Vista and cloud forest on Santo Antao, in the same archipelago.
  • Pico do Fogo and Santo AntaoFogo's cone, and Santo Antao's ridges where the paths run along the crest.
  • Seasonal OnlyThe rain comes in August and September, and the ribeiras run for a few weeks.
  • RibeirasSteep valleys terraced for sugarcane and coffee, and irrigated by hand-cut channels.
  • Sal, Boa Vista and TarrafalWhite sand and constant wind on Sal and Boa Vista, and quiet bays on Santiago.
  • The Cloud Forest of Santo AntaoThe north of Santo Antao catches enough cloud to grow coffee and dragon trees.
Wildlife
  • Turtles and HumpbacksBoa Vista is one of the largest loggerhead nesting sites in the world.
  • Sea, Not SavannaNo large land animals. What there is instead is turtles, whales and seabirds.
  • Turtle Patrols on Boa VistaCommunity and NGO patrols on the nesting beaches, and the numbers have recovered.
  • Walking Santo AntaoCobbled paths from the ridge down to the sea, a day at a time, between villages.
People and culture
  • Creole, and the MornaA Portuguese-African creole culture, and a music that put these islands on the map.
  • The Badiu and the SampadjuduThe Santiago interior and the northern islands, with different histories and different music.
  • Mindelo Carnival and Baia das GatasCarnival in February and a music festival on the beach in August.
  • Pottery, Panu and GrogueThe panu woven cloth of Santiago, Fogo's pottery, and cane spirit stills.
  • Cachupa and GrogueCachupa — the slow-cooked maize and bean stew — and grogue distilled from cane.
  • Beaches and Short FlightsCalm water on Sal and Boa Vista, short hops between islands, and a very easy pace.
  • The Bar at MidnightMindelo's bars start late and the morna does not begin until they are full.
Places
  • Praia and MindeloA clifftop capital on Santiago, and the port city on Sao Vicente where the music lives.
  • Portuguese Colonial and Volcanic StonePastel colonial in Mindelo, and black volcanic stone houses on Fogo.
  • Cidade VelhaThe first European colonial town in the tropics, founded in 1462, with the fort above it.
  • Cidade Velha, ListedInscribed in 2009 — the pillory, the cathedral ruins and the street plan of the first town.
Ways to travel
  • Kitesurfing and Ridge WalkingSal and Boa Vista for wind, and Santo Antao for some of the best walking in the Atlantic.
  • Well EstablishedResorts on Sal and Boa Vista, and small good hotels on Santo Antao and Fogo.
  • Volcano and VineCha das Caldeiras at first light, with vines growing in black ash under the cone.
  • BravaThe smallest inhabited island, reached only by boat, with flowers and no airport worth the name.
