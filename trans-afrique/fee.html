What you are buying

In your journey fee.

What Afrinkong earns, what Afrinkong arranges, and what stays yours. Set out before anything is held.

In the journey fee

  • Private ground transport for the whole crossing
  • A driver-host, and specialists along the route
  • Border-crossing coordination and permits
  • Journey coordination from arrival to departure
  • Park and attraction arrangements
  • 24/7 support while you are on the road

Arranged by us, at cost

  • Accommodation, chosen rather than searched for
  • Park, conservation and permit fees
  • Domestic flights where a leg is better flown
  • Special access and activities

Yours

  • International flights
  • Visas and entry authorisation
  • Travel and medical insurance
  • Your own meals and personal expenses

Bands are the Afrinkong journey fee — what we earn for building and running the crossing. Accommodation, park fees, permits and domestic flights are arranged by us and passed through at cost; international flights, visas, insurance and your own meals are yours. Every crossing is quoted as a whole, in writing, before anything is held.

A crossing is roughly two years of preparing for it, and the preparing is the part you can start today — start planning your journey.
