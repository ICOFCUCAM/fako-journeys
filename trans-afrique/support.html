The team

Some journeys need a guide. Trans Afrique needs a team.

Six disciplines, assembled per crossing. The route decides who travels.

Not every journey needs every specialist. The route determines the team — and the team is assembled per crossing, not kept on a bus.

01

Medical

Where the itinerary, the group size and the destination call for it.

  • Physician
  • Nurse or paramedic

02

Mobility

The vehicles, the roads, the borders and what to do when one of them closes.

  • Professional drivers
  • Vehicle technician

03

Security

Assessed per route and per season, and arranged locally and lawfully or not at all.

  • Security professionals where appropriate

04

Cultural

People who can tell you what you are looking at, in the language it is spoken in.

  • Historian or archaeologist
  • Translator or language specialist

05

Wildlife and nature

Trackers and naturalists for the stretches where that is the point.

  • Wildlife and safari specialists

06

Journey operations

The person whose whole job is that the month works, and the ones who record it.

  • Journey director
  • Photographer or filmmaker
  • Chef or culinary specialist on selected expeditions

When the route demands more

On selected crossings a medical professional accompanies the expedition. The level of support is set by the itinerary, the group size, the route and the conditions — and it is confirmed for your crossing before you book.

It is expedition support in addition to travel and medical insurance and to local emergency services, never in place of either. Every traveller carries their own cover, and that requirement does not change because a doctor is on the crossing.
