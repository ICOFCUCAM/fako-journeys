Togo

Fifty kilometres wide, and a different country every hour north.

A narrow strip from the Atlantic to the Sahel: palm coast, the Kloto hills with the country's best walking, and the fortified Batammariba houses at Koutammakou that are a World Heritage site.

What Togo is known for.

Nature & Landscapes

The Kloto Hills

Coffee and cocoa country in the southwest, forested and cool, around Kpalime.

Wildlife

Fazao-Malfakassa

Elephant, buffalo and a great many butterflies in the largest park in the country.

Mountains

Mont Agou

986 metres, the highest point, walkable in a morning from Kpalime.

Beaches & Coast

Fifty Kilometres of Coast

Palm-backed Atlantic sand from Lome east to Aneho, with a heavy swell.

Forests & Rainforest

The Kloto Forest

Semi-deciduous forest above Kpalime, and one of the richest butterfly sites in West Africa.

Culture

Ewe in the south, Kabye in the centre, Batammariba in the far northwest.

Landscapes
  • The Kloto HillsCoffee and cocoa country in the southwest, forested and cool, around Kpalime.
  • Mont Agou986 metres, the highest point, walkable in a morning from Kpalime.

  • Lake Togo and the MonoA shallow lagoon behind the coast, and the Mono river marking the Benin border.

Wildlife
  • Fazao-MalfakassaElephant, buffalo and a great many butterflies in the largest park in the country.
  • Small and RecoveringThe parks here are being rebuilt after decades of pressure, and the wildlife with them.
  • Butterflies at KlotoOver five hundred species recorded in one forest, and a local guiding tradition around them.
  • Mont Agou at DawnUp before six, and Ghana visible across the valley by seven.
People and culture
  • Ewe, Kabye, Tem, BatammaribaEwe in the south, Kabye in the centre, Batammariba in the far northwest.
  • The BatammaribaTheir name means 'those who shape the earth', and the houses are the argument.
  • Evala and the Voodoo MarketKabye wrestling in July, and the Akodessewa fetish market in Lome all year.
  • Batik and PotteryBatik and wood carving around Kpalime, and pottery from the north.
  • Fufu and Gboma DessiFufu with groundnut sauce, and gboma dessi — spinach stew with fish.
  • Beach and Waterfall in a DayTogo is narrow enough that the coast and the Kloto hills are the same short trip.
  • The Grand Marche and the Nana BenzThe women who built the wax-print trade ran it from this market, and still do.
Places
  • LomeThe only capital on a border, with the biggest market in the region behind the beach.
  • Towers of MudTakienta houses with granaries on top, and German colonial stone in Lome and Aneho.
  • Aneho and the Old CoastThe first colonial capital, with German and Afro-Brazilian buildings going quietly to seed.
  • Koutammakou, ListedThe Batammariba landscape has been a World Heritage site since 2004, and is still lived in.
Ways to travel
  • Walking Around KpalimeWaterfalls, coffee farms, Mont Agou and the butterfly forest, all within an hour.
  • A HandfulBeach hotels at Lome and a few good places at Kpalime.
  • Earth ArchitectureKoutammakou late in the day, when the mud goes gold and the granaries throw shadows.
  • Aneho and Lake TogoAn hour east of Lome: a lagoon, a decayed colonial town, and no visitors.
