Algeria

The largest country in Africa, and the emptiest.

Four-fifths Sahara, a Mediterranean coast of Roman cities, and the Hoggar and Tassili massifs in the deep south — rock art nine thousand years old on a plateau the size of Britain.

What Algeria is known for.

Nature & Landscapes

Hoggar and Tassili

Volcanic spires in the Hoggar, and a sandstone plateau at Tassili n'Ajjer eroded into streets.

Wildlife

What Lives in the Sand

Fennec fox, addax and the last Saharan cheetah, all of them nocturnal and all of them rare.

Mountains

The Djurdjura and the Hoggar

Snow on the Djurdjura above Kabylie in winter; Mount Tahat at 2,908 metres in the far south.

Beaches & Coast

A Thousand Kilometres of Mediterranean

The Turquoise Coast west of Algiers, and the coves at Jijel under limestone cliffs.

Forests & Rainforest

The Kabylie Cedars

Atlas cedar on the Djurdjura ridge, some of it four hundred years old.

Culture
