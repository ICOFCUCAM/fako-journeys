Burkina Faso

The best cinema and the best masks in West Africa.

FESPACO is the largest film festival on the continent, the Sindou peaks are a forest of eroded sandstone, and the mask traditions of the west are among the strongest anywhere.

Landscapes
  • The SouthwestCascades, sugarcane and the Karfiguela falls where the land drops off the plateau.
  • Tenakourou747 metres in the southwest, and the highest ground in a country that is mostly plateau.
  • KarfiguelaWater off the plateau in a series of ledges, swimmable at the bottom.
  • The Black and White VoltaTwo of the three Voltas rise here and run south into Ghana.
  • Lakeshore, Not CoastLandlocked. Tengrela and the Bagre reservoir are where people swim and fish.
  • Gallery Forest at BanforaThe dome-shaped Sindou woodland, and the fields of sugarcane below the cliffs.
Wildlife
  • Hippos at TengrelaA lake outside Banfora with a resident hippo pod, worked by fishermen in pirogues.
  • Nazinga and ArliNazinga has one of the densest elephant populations in West Africa and you can walk in it.
  • NazingaA community-run ranch since 1979, and one of the earliest of its kind in the region.
  • The Sindou RidgeScrambling routes through the fins, best in the last two hours of light.
People and culture
  • Sixty Languages, One CinemaFESPACO in Ouagadougou every two years, and a film industry that punches far above its size.
  • Mossi, Bobo, Lobi, FulaniThe Mossi kingdoms in the centre, the Bobo and Lobi in the west, the Fulani across the north.
  • FESPACO and FESTIMAThe film festival in Ouagadougou, and the mask festival at Dedougou every other year.
  • Bronze and MasksLost-wax bronze in Ouagadougou, and the plank masks of the Bwa and Nuna.
  • To and Riz GrasTo — millet paste with sauce — and riz gras cooked in tomato and oil.
  • Elephants You Can Walk ToNazinga allows walking safaris, which makes it unusually good for older children.
  • The Maquis at NightOpen-air bars with grilled chicken and dolo, and a band more often than not.
Places
  • Ouagadougou and Bobo-DioulassoA capital that fills for FESPACO, and a second city with the country's best music.
  • Sudanese Mud and SukhalaThe Bobo-Dioulasso mosque in Sudanese style, and painted Kassena compounds at Tiebele.
  • The Ruins of LoropeniStone walls six metres high, a thousand years old, from the trans-Saharan gold trade.
  • Loropeni, and the Living RestOne World Heritage site in stone, and a mask tradition that never became a museum.
Ways to travel
  • Cycling the SouthwestBurkina is a cycling nation. The Tour du Faso runs every year and the roads are flat.
  • ModestGood hotels in Ouagadougou and Bobo, and a lodge or two near Banfora.
  • Masks in MotionFESTIMA, where the masks are danced rather than displayed and the dust is part of it.
  • TiebeleKassena compounds painted in black, white and ochre geometry by the women of the household.
