Benin

Where vodun comes from, and the palaces of Dahomey.

Ouidah is the source of vodun and a departure point of the Atlantic slave trade, Abomey holds the earthen palaces of the kings of Dahomey, and Ganvie is a town of thirty thousand people on stilts in a lagoon.

  • Lake Nokoue and the OuemeThe lagoon behind Cotonou, and the Oueme running down to it through the delta.

Wildlife
  • PendjariLion, elephant, buffalo and cheetah in one of the last strongholds of the West African lion.
  • The W-Arly-Pendjari ComplexThe largest intact ecosystem in West Africa, run across three countries.
  • Pendjari, Community-RunManaged since 2017 by African Parks with the villages around it, and the lion count is up.
  • Walking the AtakoraSomba villages, quartzite ridges and the Tanougou pool, on foot from Natitingou.
People and culture
  • Where Vodun Comes FromNot a folk survival — a living religion with millions of followers, and a public holiday.
  • Fon, Yoruba, Bariba, SombaThe Fon of Dahomey in the south, the Somba in the Atakora with their fortified houses.
  • The Vodun Festival10 January at Ouidah, on the beach, and it is a religious event before it is a spectacle.
  • Applique and BronzeThe Abomey applique banners that recorded the kings' reigns, and Fon bronze casting.
  • Amiwo and Grilled FishAmiwo — maize with tomato and chicken — and fish straight off the Grand-Popo beach.
  • Ganvie by PirogueA whole town on water, reached by boat, which no child has ever found boring.
  • ZemidjanThe motorcycle taxis are the transport system, and their yellow shirts are the uniform.
Places
  • Cotonou, Porto-Novo, AbomeyA port that never stops, a colonial capital, and the seat of a kingdom.
  • Earth and Afro-BrazilianSomba tata towers in the north, and Afro-Brazilian merchant houses in Ouidah and Porto-Novo.
  • The Royal Palaces of AbomeyTwelve palaces of the kings of Dahomey, in earth, with bas-reliefs telling the history.
  • The Route of the SlavesFour kilometres from Ouidah's auction square to the Door of No Return on the beach.
Ways to travel
  • Pirogue and PendjariPoling through Ganvie in the morning, and a game drive in the north two days later.
  • A Few Good OnesLodges at Pendjari and on the coast at Grand-Popo, and good hotels in Cotonou.
  • Stilts and ReflectionGanvie at first light, when the water is still and the houses double.
  • Possotome and Lake AhemeA quiet lake west of Ouidah with fishing villages and hot springs on its shore.
