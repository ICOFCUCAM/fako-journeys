Mauritius

Reef, sugar and the volcanic interior.

A volcanic island inside an almost unbroken reef, with a mountainous interior, Black River Gorges, and a culture assembled from India, Africa, France and China in the space of two centuries.

People and culture
  • Four ContinentsIndian, African, French and Chinese, in one Creole language and one calendar.

Places
