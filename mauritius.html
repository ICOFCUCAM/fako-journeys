Mauritius. Reef, sugar and the volcanic interior.

A volcanic island inside an almost unbroken reef, with a mountainous interior, Black River Gorges, and a culture assembled from India, Africa, France and China in the space of two centuries.

01Highlights

What Mauritius is known for.

Six of the twenty-seven, and the ones most people come for. The rest are below — and none of them is filler.

Nature & Landscapes

Lagoon and Gorge

Lagoon all the way round, sugar cane across the plain, and gorges cut into the plateau.

Wildlife

What the Dodo Left

The dodo is gone, but the pink pigeon and the kestrel were both brought back from single figures.

Mountains

Le Morne and Pieter Both

Le Morne Brabant stands 556 metres out of the lagoon, and is walked in a morning.

Beaches & Coast

The Lagoon

Inside the reef the water is flat and warm the whole way round the island.

Forests & Rainforest

Black River Gorges

The last of the native forest, on the plateau, with trails and endemic birds in it.

Culture

Four Continents

Indian, African, French and Chinese, in one Creole language and one calendar.

03Why go

The case for Mauritius.

Why Visit This Country

Four Cultures, One Lagoon

A Hindu festival, a Creole kitchen, a French road name and a Chinese shop, in one street.

Hidden Gems

Rodrigues

Six hundred kilometres east, a tenth the size, and the island Mauritius used to be.

Eco Tourism

Bringing Back the Birds

The kestrel, the pink pigeon and the echo parakeet were each brought back from near zero.

Heritage

Le Morne

The mountain where escaped slaves lived at the top, and a world heritage site for that reason.
