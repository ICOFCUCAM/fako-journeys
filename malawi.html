Malawi

A lake with more fish species than any other.

Lake Malawi holds more freshwater fish species than any lake on earth, Liwonde and Majete have been restocked to the point of holding all the big five, and the Mulanje massif rises three thousand metres straight off the plain.

  • Mulanje3,002 metres at Sapitwa, a granite massif with huts, paths and a week of walking on it.

  • The ShireThe lake's only outlet, running south through Liwonde to the Zambezi.

Wildlife
  • Cichlids, and the Big FiveHundreds of endemic cichlids in the lake, and the big five back in the parks on shore.
  • Liwonde and MajeteBoth restocked from near-empty — Majete had nothing in 2003 and has the big five now.
  • Majete's RecoveryAfrican Parks took on an empty park in 2003 and it has the big five and no poaching now.
  • Mulanje for a WeekHut to hut across the massif, with porters, and paths that have been there a century.
People and culture
  • The Warm HeartThe reputation for friendliness is real, and it is the first thing every visitor mentions.
  • Chewa, Yao, Tumbuka, LomweThe Chewa Gule Wamkulu masquerade is on the UNESCO list and is danced still.
  • The Lake of StarsA music festival on the shore, and Gule Wamkulu in the villages all year.
  • Carving and ChitenjeBlackwood carving, basketry, and the printed chitenje cloth worn everywhere.
  • Nsima and ChamboNsima with relish, and chambo — a lake cichlid — grilled whole on the shore.
  • Easy, and WarmShort distances, no malaria-free claim but a very manageable country with children.
  • The Beach at Cape MaclearFishermen, boats, children and visitors on the same strip of sand all day.
Places
  • Lilongwe and BlantyreA quiet capital, and an older commercial city with the country's first church in it.
  • Mission Stone and Lakeshore BrickScottish mission churches in stone, and fired brick along the lake.
  • LivingstoniaA mission on the escarpment built in 1894, with the manse, the church and the view intact.
  • Lake Malawi National ParkThe first freshwater national park in the world, listed for its cichlids.
Ways to travel
  • Diving, Kayak and the MassifFreshwater diving on the cichlid reefs, kayaking the lake shore, and Mulanje for a week.
  • Small and Well RunLakeshore lodges, Mkulumadzi at Majete, and Kaya Mawa on Likoma island.
  • Clear Water and GraniteThe lake in September, when the water is at its clearest and the boulders go down twenty metres.
  • Likoma IslandA Malawian island inside Mozambican water, with a cathedral the size of Winchester's.
