  • Mount NimbaThe Nimba range on the Guinea and Liberia border, at 1,752 metres, with its own species.
  • Man and the WaterfallsThe falls around Man in the western hills, and the liana bridges strung above them.
  • The Ébrié and the BandamaThe lagoon system behind the coast, and the Bandama running the length of the country.
  • Assinie and Grand-BérébyAssinie's sandbar east of Abidjan, and the quieter Atlantic beaches to the west.

Wildlife
  • Taï and ComoéPygmy hippo and chimpanzees at Taï, and Comoé's savanna in the north-east.
  • ComoéOne of the largest protected areas in West Africa, with elephant, buffalo and hippo.
  • Taï and the ChimpanzeesForty years of chimpanzee research at Taï, run with the villages around the park.
  • Surf and HikeAssinie's breaks, and the liana bridges and falls in the hills around Man.
People and culture
  • Sixty PeoplesAkan, Krou, Mandé and Gur — around sixty peoples inside one border.
  • Baoulé, Senufo and DanBaoulé weaving, Senufo sculpture, and Dan masks from the western hills.
  • Abissa and the MasksAbissa at Grand-Bassam in November, and the mask festivals of the western hills.
  • Weaving and CarvingBaoulé strip cloth, Korhogo painted cloth, and Dan masks carved for use rather than sale.
  • Attiéké and KedjenouAttiéké with grilled fish, and kedjenou slow-cooked in a sealed pot.
  • Beach and LagoonAssinie's sandbar has ocean on one side and calm lagoon on the other, which suits children.
  • Maquis at NightThe open-air maquis — grill, music and a plastic chair — is the country's real institution.
Places
  • AbidjanThe Plateau's towers, the Ébrié lagoon, and a nightlife the region measures itself against.
  • Lagoon ColonialThe wooden trading houses of Grand-Bassam, and Abidjan's tropical modernism.
  • Grand-BassamThe first colonial capital, on a sandbar between lagoon and ocean, and now a world site.
  • YamoussoukroA basilica larger than Saint Peter's, built in the founder's home village.
Ways to travel
  • Surf, Hike, PaddleSurf at Assinie, hiking the western hills, and pirogues through the lagoon.
  • Assinie and AbidjanLagoon villas at Assinie, and Abidjan hotels that serve the whole region's business.
  • Lagoon LightPirogues on flat water at either end of the day, and a skyline behind them.
  • ManThe western hills, with waterfalls, mask villages and a mountain nobody climbs.
