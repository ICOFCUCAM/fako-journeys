Chad

Zakouma, and the lakes of Ounianga.

Zakouma brought its elephants back from four hundred and fifty to over six hundred, the Ennedi is a sandstone massif full of rock art and desert crocodiles, and the Ounianga lakes sit in the middle of the Sahara without a river feeding them.

Landscapes
  • The EnnediA sandstone massif eroded into arches, towers and canyons, with water at the bottom of them.
  • Emi Koussi and the TibestiA 3,415-metre volcano in the far north, the highest point in the Sahara.
  • None, and Guelta InsteadNo falls. What the Ennedi has is the Guelta d'Archei, a permanent gorge pool.
  • Lake Chad and the Ounianga LakesA shrinking inland sea in the west, and eighteen lakes in the Sahara that no river feeds.
  • Landlocked, and the LakeThe beaches here are the shores of Lake Chad, worked by fishermen in reed boats.
  • Gallery Forest on the RiversThe Chari and Logone carry woodland far north into the Sahel.
Wildlife
  • Buffalo in ThousandsZakouma's buffalo move in herds of over a thousand, and the Kordofan giraffe with them.
  • Zakouma in MarchEverything concentrates on the last pans, and the birdlife with it — crowned crane in their thousands.
  • The Turnaround at ZakoumaAfrican Parks took it on in 2010 after poaching had taken ninety per cent of the elephants.
  • Walking ZakoumaZakouma allows walking with armed rangers, which very few West and Central African parks do.
People and culture
  • Two Hundred GroupsToubou and Zaghawa in the north, Sara in the south, and Arabic and French across both.
  • The ToubouA desert people of the Tibesti and Ennedi, and among the most self-sufficient anywhere.
  • The Gerewol and the Camel RacesWodaabe gatherings in the Sahel, and camel racing in the desert north.
  • Leatherwork and SilverToubou leather, Sara basketry, and silver from the N'Djamena market.
  • Boule and Grilled CapitaineMillet boule with sauce, and Nile perch off the Chari grilled on the bank.
  • Serious CountryDistances are enormous and facilities are few. Zakouma is possible; the Ennedi is an expedition.
  • Tea in the ShadeThree glasses, poured from a height, and no business concluded before the third.
Places
  • N'DjamenaA capital on the Chari, opposite Cameroon, with the largest market in the region.
  • Mud and BancoSahelian mud-brick in the towns, and reed and mat construction on the lake.
  • Ennedi Rock ArtThousands of painted and engraved panels recording a Sahara with cattle and giraffe in it.
  • Two Sites in the SaharaThe Ennedi massif and the Ounianga lakes, both World Heritage, both barely visited.
Ways to travel
  • Crossing the EnnediTen days by four-wheel-drive between arches and gueltas, sleeping under the rock.
  • One Camp, and It Is GoodZakouma has a single seasonal camp. The Ennedi is tents you bring with you.
  • Sandstone and ShadowThe Ennedi arches in the last hour, when the sandstone goes from orange to red.
  • The Guelta d'ArcheiCamels come down a gorge to drink, and desert crocodiles live in the pool below them.
