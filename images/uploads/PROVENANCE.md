
## Kilimanjaro, 15 August — three from Pexels, not from a camera of ours

`kilimanjaro-elephants-acacia`, `kilimanjaro-above-the-cloud` and
`kilimanjaro-game-drive` arrived as `pexels-laukevtravel-26924191.jpg`,
`pexels-marri-shyam-366418-7463697.jpg` and `pexels-twilightkenya-14510937.jpg`.

They are in this folder because everything here is a real photograph rather than
a generated one, which is the distinction the folder exists to make. They are
not the operator's own work, and the original filenames are recorded above so
that stays checkable: the photographers are credited on Pexels as laukevtravel,
marri-shyam and twilightkenya, under the Pexels licence.

**Where they were taken matters more than usual here.** Kilimanjaro is in
Tanzania; this view of it is from Amboseli, on the Kenyan side — the game drive
frame has a Kenyan number plate in it. So a caption may say Kilimanjaro, because
that is the mountain. A caption may not imply the viewer is standing in
Tanzania, because whoever took these was not.
