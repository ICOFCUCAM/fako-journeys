# What is in this folder, and where it came from

Everything here is a real photograph. That is the one thing the folder
guarantees: `images/generated/` is where anything synthetic lives, and the host
a URL sits on is the proof of which is which. It does not guarantee that the
operator took the picture — several did not — so this file records what is
known, per batch, and the rule is that a name describes what is in the frame.

## Kilimanjaro, 15 August — three, from Pexels

`kilimanjaro-elephants-acacia`, `kilimanjaro-above-the-cloud` and
`kilimanjaro-game-drive` arrived as `pexels-laukevtravel-26924191.jpg`,
`pexels-marri-shyam-366418-7463697.jpg` and `pexels-twilightkenya-14510937.jpg`.
The photographers are credited on Pexels as laukevtravel, marri-shyam and
twilightkenya, under the Pexels licence.

**Where they were taken matters more than usual.** Kilimanjaro is in Tanzania;
this view of it is from Amboseli, on the Kenyan side — the game-drive frame has
a Kenyan number plate in it. A caption may say Kilimanjaro, because that is the
mountain. A caption may not imply the viewer is standing in Tanzania.

## The Nairobi batch, 15 August — and why five of them are not Nairobi

Eleven files arrived, eight of them named `Nairobi Kenya (n).jpg`. Three of
those eight were the Kilimanjaro photographs above, uploaded a second time under
a Nairobi name. Placed as Nairobi they would have captioned a Tanzanian
mountain, seen from Amboseli, as Kenya's capital — so they were dropped rather
than kept twice, and the copies already here under `kilimanjaro-` are the ones
used.

Two more are not cityscapes at all. Renamed for what they show:

    city-nairobi-skyline        Nairobi by day, towers over red-roofed blocks
    city-nairobi-towers         Nairobi's newer glass towers
    city-nairobi-night          Nairobi after dark, lit avenues to the horizon
    life-bicycle-banana-grove   two people and a bicycle on a path through
    life-bicycle-banana-grove-two   banana groves — two frames, place unknown
    life-basketball-match       a basketball game under floodlights, place unknown
    city-cameroon-aerial        a Cameroonian city from the air (from a file
                                named SSA-CMR-...; the city itself is not stated)
    city-yaounde-hills          Yaoundé across its hills

`place unknown` is not a placeholder for research nobody did. It is the reason
those four are not on any slot that names a place, and they stay off one until
somebody who was there says where.

## Douala, and why it is not here

`Douala.jpg` arrived with this batch and was not kept. Two reasons, either
sufficient on its own:

- **512x640.** The narrowest variant every other photograph here is served at is
  800px. It could not fill a card without being enlarged past its own detail.
- **A handle burnt into the frame.** `@marcelpoko` sits in the lower right. This
  repository turned a city photograph away for a TikTok handle across the frame
  before, and images/VIDEO.md sets it out as a rule: no burnt-in titles, logos,
  watermarks or handles.

The second is the one that would still apply at 4000px. If the photographer is
somebody the operator knows, an unwatermarked copy with permission is welcome and
Douala is one of the cities in cities.json still carrying `photo: null`.

`Yaounde.webp` was the same 1344x768 frame as the Yaoundé already processed from
an earlier batch, so it was dropped rather than kept twice.

## Dakar and Douala, 15 August — the last two cities

Both arrived from the operator as direct uploads, and both closed a gap: they
were the only two cities in the collection with no photograph.

**Dakar.** Two frames were sent. `city-dakar-corniche-1600w.jpg` is the blue-hour
one, cropped 3:2 from `Dakar Senegal 2.jpg` (2048x1894) — the Corniche, the round
hotel and the Plateau tower. The other, `Dakar Senegal.jpg`, is not used: it
pushes sky and sea to a cyan that measures 251 at the 90th percentile, and
images/STANDARD.md exists to catch exactly that. Both originals are kept.

**Douala.** `city-douala-bonanjo-526w.jpg` is `Douala 2.jpg` unaltered. It is
526x789, under the 800px floor every other photograph here meets, and it is used
anyway for two reasons: it is the first clean frame of that city this repository
has had — see the refusal above, for a handle burnt into the corner — and it
lands on an ordinary card rather than one of the two wide ones, where it covers
its column comfortably. It should be replaced with a larger frame when one
exists, and until then it is the honest best available.

Neither carries a credit line, because neither arrived with one. If these came
from a photographer rather than the operator's own camera, that name belongs
here.
