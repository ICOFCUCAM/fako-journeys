DR Congo

Virunga, Nyiragongo, and the okapi.

An active lava lake you can camp beside on Nyiragongo, the only mountain gorillas outside Rwanda and Uganda, the okapi that lives nowhere else, and five World Heritage sites — a country of superlatives and of serious constraints on reaching them.

Landscapes
  • The Albertine RiftGlaciers on the Rwenzori, volcanoes in the Virungas, and the deepest forest in Africa between them.
  • The Rwenzori5,109 metres at Margherita, glaciated, on the equator, and the Mountains of the Moon.
  • Boyoma and LofoiThe seven cataracts at Kisangani, and Lofoi with a single drop of 340 metres.
  • The Congo RiverFour and a half thousand kilometres, and the deepest river in the world at 220 metres.
  • Muanda, and the LakesForty kilometres of Atlantic at the river mouth, and beaches on Kivu and Tanganyika.
  • The Ituri and the SalongaSalonga is the largest tropical rainforest reserve in Africa, and mostly unentered.
Wildlife
  • Mountain Gorillas and OkapiVirunga's gorillas in the east, and the okapi in the Ituri forest, which lives nowhere else.
  • Garamba and UpembaNorthern savanna with elephant and Kordofan giraffe, being brought back after decades of loss.
  • Virunga's RangersOver two hundred rangers have died protecting this park. It is the oldest in Africa.
  • Volcano and GorillaNyiragongo, then gorillas in Virunga's lowlands, from the same base at Goma.
People and culture
  • Two Hundred and Fifty PeoplesFour national languages, and a music that has set the terms for the whole continent.
  • The MbutiForest hunter-gatherers of the Ituri, with a relationship to that forest going back millennia.
  • Rumba and NdomboloFranco, Papa Wemba, Koffi Olomide — Kinshasa has exported dance music for seventy years.
  • Masks, Raffia and CopperKuba raffia cloth, Pende and Songye masks, and Katangese copper crosses.
  • Fufu, Pondu and LibokeCassava fufu, pondu cassava leaf, and fish steamed in leaves over a fire.
  • Not for FamiliesThe east requires current advice and the distances are enormous. This is careful travel.
  • SAPEThe Society of Ambianceurs and Elegant People — dressing as a discipline and an argument.
Places
  • Kinshasa and LubumbashiOne of the largest cities in Africa, facing Brazzaville, and the mining capital in the south.
  • Belgian Colonial and CiteArt deco Kinshasa from the 1930s, and the cite quarters that grew around it.
  • Kisangani and the River StationsStanley's route up the river, and the Wagenia fishing structures that predate all of it.
  • Five World Heritage SitesVirunga, Garamba, Kahuzi-Biega, Salonga and the Okapi Reserve — all five on the danger list.
Ways to travel
  • Nyiragongo OvernightSix hours up, a night in a hut on the rim, and the lava lake below you all of it.
  • Virunga's LodgesMikeno and Tchegera, run by the park itself, and the money goes back into it.
  • Lava at NightThe Nyiragongo crater after dark, when the lake is the only light there is.
  • Lake KivuVolcanic islands, terraced hills and calm water, between Goma and Bukavu.
