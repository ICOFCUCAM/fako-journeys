Lesotho

The kingdom in the sky.

The only country entirely above a thousand metres, with Africa's highest single-drop waterfall at Maletsunyane, southern Africa's only ski resort, and dinosaur footprints in the sandstone.

Landscapes
  • The MalotiBasalt highlands cut by river gorges, and the roof of southern Africa.
  • Thabana Ntlenyana3,482 metres, the highest point in Africa south of Kilimanjaro.
  • Maletsunyane192 metres in a single drop, and a commercial abseil down beside it.
  • The SenquThe Orange river rises here and runs the whole width of South Africa afterwards.
  • High ReservoirsLandlocked and mountainous: the water here is Katse and Mohale, and it is sold to South Africa.
  • Almost NoneAbove the treeline for most of the country, and the small patches left are protected.
Wildlife
  • Bearded Vulture and ElandThe lammergeier breeds on the Drakensberg escarpment, and eland are back in the reserves.
  • Not the Business HereThis is a mountain country, not a savanna one. What you go for is the ground and the horses.
  • Water as the EconomyThe Highlands Water Project sells water to South Africa, and the dams are the landscape now.
  • Sani and SehlabathebeThe Sani Pass in four-wheel-drive, and the rock formations and rivers of Sehlabathebe.
People and culture
  • The Blanket and the HatThe Basotho blanket is worn seriously and read closely, and the mokorotlo is on the flag.
  • The BasothoOne people, one language, and a kingdom that kept its independence by going uphill.
  • Morija Arts and Cultural FestivalMusic, poetry and horse racing at Morija every year, and the country turns up.
  • Blankets, Hats and TapestryWoven mohair tapestry, the conical mokorotlo hat, and the blankets themselves.
  • Papa, Moroho and MotohoMaize papa with wild greens, and motoho — fermented sorghum drink.
  • Ponies and SnowShort pony treks, dinosaur footprints and, in winter, actual snow.
  • Sunday on HorsebackThe horse is transport, not recreation, and every village has them saddled.
Places
  • MaseruA small capital on the Caledon, with the border post at the end of the main street.
  • Sandstone and RondavelColonial sandstone in Maseru, and painted rondavels with stone walls in the mountains.
  • Thaba BosiuThe flat-topped mountain Moshoeshoe I defended, and where the nation was made.
  • Maloti-DrakensbergA World Heritage site shared with South Africa, for its landscape and its San rock art.
Ways to travel
  • Pony TrekkingThe Basotho pony is the way to cross this country, and has been for a hundred and fifty years.
  • Lodges and Trading PostsMalealea and Semonkong grew out of trading posts, and they are the best of it.
  • High and ClearThe Maloti in autumn, when the grass turns and the air will not hold any haze.
  • Dinosaur FootprintsTrackways in the sandstone at Moyeni and Morija, in the open and unfenced.
