04Where to start

Two countries that each contain a continent.

If you have never been, start with one of these. Both are unusually varied for their size, both are straightforward to travel, and both are ours to run end to end.

Africa in miniature

Cameroon

One country, six climates. From black sand at sea level to 4,095 metres of active volcano, through the Korup rainforest and the highland kingdoms to the savanna at Waza — a transect of the whole continent in nine hundred kilometres. Run by Kamerun, out of Buea and Douala.

Enter Kamerun →
The Pearl of Africa

Uganda

Where wildlife, wilderness and adventure meet. Mountain gorillas in Bwindi, the Rwenzori above five thousand metres, the Nile forced through six metres of rock at Murchison, and the crater lakes of the west. Green in a way the rest of the safari circuit is not. Run by Pearl Trails Uganda, out of Kampala.
