01Explore Africa

Five regions. Thousands of ways to discover Africa.

Africa is not one destination. “Africa” covers a landmass you could fit the United States, China, India and most of Europe inside. Start with the region, discover the country, then find the experience that belongs to you.

Central & West

Rainforest, volcanic coast, savanna, ancient kingdoms and the cultures of the Gulf of Guinea.

Cameroon · Ghana · more coming

East Africa

The Rift, great migrations, gorilla forests, Kilimanjaro, the Nile and the Swahili coast.

Uganda · Kenya · Tanzania · Rwanda

Southern Africa

The Namib, Zambezi, Kalahari, Cape and some of the continent’s greatest wilderness.

Namibia · Zambia · South Africa

North Africa

The Atlas, Sahara, Mediterranean cities, ancient civilisations and the Nile.

Morocco · more coming

Islands

Indian Ocean beaches, volcanic islands, tropical forests and cultures shaped by the sea.

Madagascar · Seychelles · Mauritius · Comoros

04Two places to start

Two countries that each contain a continent.

If you have never been, start with one of these. Both are unusually varied for their size, both are straightforward to travel, and both are ours to run end to end.

Africa in miniature

Cameroon

One country, six climates. From Atlantic black-sand beaches to the 4,095-metre summit of Mount Cameroon, through rainforest, highland kingdoms and northern savanna. Run by Kamerun, out of Buea and Douala.

Enter Cameroon →
The Pearl of Africa

Uganda

Where wildlife, wilderness and adventure meet. Mountain gorillas in Bwindi, chimpanzees in Kibale, the Nile forced through six metres of rock at Murchison, Queen Elizabeth National Park and the Rwenzori. Run by Pearl Trails Uganda, out of Kampala.
