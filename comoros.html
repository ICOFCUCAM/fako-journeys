Comoros

An active volcano, and the fish that came back from the dead.

Karthala is one of the most active volcanoes in the world with a crater four kilometres across, the coelacanth was found alive off these islands after eighty million years, and Moroni's stone town is the least visited Swahili port on the coast.

Landscapes
  • Three Islands and a ClaimGrande Comore, Anjouan and Moheli, each volcanic, each different, and Mayotte disputed.
  • The Volcanic InteriorKarthala at 2,361 metres on Grande Comore, and Ntringui on Anjouan.
  • Anjouan's CascadesThe Dziancoundre falls, and streams off the interior that run all year.
  • No Rivers on Grande ComoreThe lava is too porous to hold a river — the water is in cisterns and Lake Sale.
  • Black Sand and WhiteBlack volcanic sand on Grande Comore, and white on Moheli and Anjouan.
  • The Last ForestKarthala's forest, and Moheli's, hold the bats, the flying fox and the endemic birds.
Wildlife
  • Livingstone's Fruit BatA metre and a half across, found on Anjouan and Moheli and nowhere else.
  • Moheli Marine ParkTurtles nesting in numbers, dugong, and humpbacks calving between July and October.
  • Moheli's Community ParkThe marine park is run with the villages, and the turtle numbers have held because of it.
  • Volcano and ReefKarthala overnight, and diving the drop-offs on the same trip.
People and culture
  • Swahili, and Ylang-YlangA Swahili island culture, and most of the world's ylang-ylang for perfume.
  • The Grand MariageThe anda — a marriage that confers social rank, and can take a decade to afford.
  • The Grand Mariage SeasonWeddings run through the dry season and take over whole villages for days.
  • Woodwork, Gold and EmbroideryCarved doors, gold wedding jewellery, and the embroidered kofia cap.
  • Langouste and PilaoLobster off the reef, pilao rice with cardamom, and vanilla and clove in everything.
  • Turtles and Shallow ReefMoheli's nesting beaches, and reef close enough in to snorkel from the sand.
  • The Harbour at MoroniDhows unloading onto the rocks, and the mosque directly behind them.
Places
  • Moroni and MutsamuduA volcanic harbour capital, and Anjouan's stone town under a citadel.
  • Swahili StoneCoral-stone houses with carved doors, and the mosque architecture of the whole coast.
  • The Medina of MoroniA Swahili stone town with the Old Friday Mosque on the harbour since 1427.
  • Nothing Listed, Much StandingNo World Heritage sites, and Swahili stone towns on all three islands.
Ways to travel
  • Climbing KarthalaOvernight from Boboni, camping on the rim, and down the next day.
  • Small and SimpleGuesthouses and a few beach hotels. Nothing at the top end anywhere.
  • Volcano and OceanKarthala's rim at first light, with the Indian Ocean on every side of it.
  • The CoelacanthCaught off these islands in 1938, having been extinct for eighty million years.
