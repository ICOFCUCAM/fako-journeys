South Sudan

The Sudd, and the second-largest migration on earth.

A million white-eared kob moving across Boma and Badingilo, the Sudd wetland the size of Belgium, and the cattle camps of the Mundari — the youngest country in the world, and the least travelled.

Landscapes
  • The SuddOne of the largest wetlands on earth, where the Nile spreads out and slows almost to a stop.
  • The ImatongMount Kinyeti at 3,187 metres on the Ugandan border, the highest point in the country.
  • Fula FallsThe Nile dropping through rock at Fula, on its way north out of Uganda.
  • The White NileIt runs the length of the country and it is the reason for everything here.
  • No Coast, Plenty of WaterLandlocked, but the river islands and sandbars near Terekeka do the job.
  • The Southern ForestsGuinea-Congolian rainforest at the Congo border, with forest elephant and chimpanzee in it.
Wildlife
  • The Great Kob MigrationWhite-eared kob, tiang and Mongalla gazelle — counted at over a million animals.
  • Boma and BadingiloTwo parks either side of the migration route, with almost no infrastructure and no crowds.
  • The Sudd Holds the Nile BackHalf the water entering the Sudd evaporates in it. The wetland is a climate system.
  • Walking the ImatongCool forest, waterfalls and Kinyeti, from Gilo. Guides are essential and available.
People and culture
  • Sixty-Four PeoplesDinka, Nuer, Shilluk, Bari, Azande, Mundari and fifty-eight more, in a country of twelve million.
  • The MundariCattle are wealth, name and argument. A camp of them at dusk is unlike anything else.
  • WrestlingDinka and Mundari wrestling matches draw whole districts, with drumming and singing round the ring.
  • Beadwork and CorsetsDinka beaded corsets in colours that state a man's age set, and Azande basketry.
  • Kisra, Asida and FishSorghum asida, kisra flatbread, and Nile perch grilled on the bank.
  • Hard Country for ItThis is not a family destination yet, and pretending otherwise would not help anyone.
  • Market Day at TerekekaCattle, sorghum, fish and tea, and the whole district in one place.
Places
  • JubaA river port that became a capital in 2011 and has been building ever since.
  • TukulsRound mud walls, conical thatch, and a shape that has not needed improving.
  • Lado and the Old PostsThe Lado Enclave, Gondokoro and the river stations of the nineteenth-century Nile expeditions.
  • Nothing on the List YetNo World Heritage sites, and a heritage that is living rather than ruined.
Ways to travel
  • Getting to BomaLight aircraft and then hours of track. It is expedition travel and it is honest about that.
  • Two CampsThere is a mobile camp on the migration and very little else. That is the honest position.
  • Smoke and HornsA Mundari camp at first light, when the fires are lit and the light comes through them.
  • NimuleA small park on the Ugandan border with elephant, and the Nile going through it.
