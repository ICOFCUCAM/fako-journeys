The human layer

Africa is not a backdrop.

Every country here was written up through the same twenty-seven headings, and twelve of them are about people. So the same question can be put to all 22 at once — and it comes back 22 different ways, which is the part a photograph of a sunset cannot tell you. 7 questions, 154 answers, none of them about “African culture” in general.

Pick a country to put all seven to one place.

Who lives here?

Africa is not one people. It is roughly two thousand languages, and the answer changes at every border and often inside one.

Four large cultures and hundreds of smaller ones, each with its own courts and calendars.
