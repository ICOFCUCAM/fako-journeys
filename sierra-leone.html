Nature & Landscapes

The Loma Mountains

Cloud forest and montane grassland in the northeast, hardly walked at all.

Wildlife

Eleven Primates at Tiwai

An island in the Moa river with one of the highest primate densities anywhere.

Mountains

Bintumani

1,945 metres in the Loma range, the highest point in West Africa west of Cameroon.

Beaches & Coast

River No. 2 and the Banana Islands

White sand, forest behind, and two small islands an hour offshore with reef around them.

Gola Rainforest

The largest remaining block of Upper Guinean forest, now a transboundary park with Liberia.

Culture

Landscapes
  • The Loma MountainsCloud forest and montane grassland in the northeast, hardly walked at all.
  • Bintumani1,945 metres in the Loma range, the highest point in West Africa west of Cameroon.
  • Charlotte and BumbunaCharlotte falls in the peninsula forest, and Bumbuna on the Rokel in the interior.
  • The Moa and the RokelThe Moa runs past Tiwai, and the Rokel comes down to the estuary at Freetown.
  • River No. 2 and the Banana IslandsWhite sand, forest behind, and two small islands an hour offshore with reef around them.
  • Gola RainforestThe largest remaining block of Upper Guinean forest, now a transboundary park with Liberia.
Wildlife
  • Eleven Primates at TiwaiAn island in the Moa river with one of the highest primate densities anywhere.
  • Forest, Not PlainPygmy hippo, forest elephant and bongo in Gola — a forest circuit, not a savanna one.
  • Tiwai, Community-RunEight villages own the island reserve and have done since before the war.
  • Climbing BintumaniFour days from Sinekoro, through forest and out onto grassland above the cloud.
People and culture
  • Krio, and Sixteen OthersFreetown was founded by freed slaves, and Krio is the language and the culture that came of it.
  • Mende, Temne and KrioMende in the south, Temne in the north, and the Krio of the peninsula.
  • Lantern ParadeIlluminated floats through Freetown on the night of Independence Day.
  • Country Cloth and GaraHand-woven country cloth strips, and gara tie-dye in indigo and kola.
  • Cassava Leaf and Country RiceCassava leaf with palm oil, groundnut stew, and fish straight off the beach.
  • Beaches With ShadeRiver No. 2 and Tokeh have forest behind the sand and calm water in the dry season.
  • Sunday at LumleyThe whole city goes to the beach, and the bars along it play until late.
Places
  • FreetownKrio board houses on the hills, the estuary below, and mountains directly behind.
  • Krio Board HousesTimber-framed, shuttered, verandahed — an architecture that came back across the Atlantic.
  • Bunce IslandA slaving fort in the estuary, ruined and overgrown, and among the most affecting in Africa.
  • The Cotton Tree, and What FollowedFreetown grew around one tree where freed slaves gave thanks in 1792.
Ways to travel
  • Surfing and Forest WalkingBureh has a surf club run by the village, and Tiwai has night walks for pottos.
  • GrowingGood beach lodges on the peninsula, and Tiwai's community camp inland.
  • Forest Meeting SandThe peninsula beaches in the late afternoon, when the hills behind them go blue.
  • The Turtle IslandsEight sand islands in the far south, reachable only by boat, with no electricity.
