Somalia

The longest coastline in mainland Africa.

Three thousand kilometres of Indian Ocean, the painted rock shelters at Laas Geel that are among the oldest in Africa, and the Cal Madow mountains — a country with real destinations and real constraints on reaching them.

Landscapes
  • Cal MadowA mountain range behind Bosaso with cloud forest on top and frankincense on the slopes.
  • Shimbiris2,460 metres in the Cal Madow, the highest point, with juniper and cycads near the top.
  • Seasonal, and SuddenThe Shabelle and Juba run south; everything else runs for a fortnight after the rains.
  • The Juba and the ShabelleTwo rivers off the Ethiopian highlands that make the only irrigated farmland in the country.
  • Berbera and BeyondBerbera's beaches on the Gulf of Aden, and a reef wall a short swim from the sand.
  • Frankincense CountryBoswellia trees on the Cal Madow slopes, tapped as they have been for four thousand years.
Wildlife
  • Speke's Gazelle and the SeaSpeke's gazelle and dibatag inland, and turtles, dugong and whale sharks offshore.
  • Not the CircuitThere are no game lodges. The wildlife here is coastal, and the interior is for those who know it.
  • Frankincense and the LandBoswellia grows on bare limestone where nothing else will, and it holds the slope together.
  • Walking the Cal MadowCool forest, cycads and views to the Gulf of Aden, from Erigavo.
People and culture
  • Poetry as the National ArtSomalis are called a nation of poets, and the reputation is deserved and enforced.
  • Nomad and PastoralistCamel herding is the oldest way of life here and still a large part of the economy.
  • Weddings and BuraanburBuraanbur — women's poetry with drumming and dance — carries the celebration.
  • Weaving and WoodcarvingAqal nomadic house frames, carved wooden headrests, and woven mats from the north.
  • Camel Milk and BariisCamel milk, bariis iskukaris spiced with cumin and cardamom, and Somali tea with ginger.
  • Beaches, and CareLido beach in Mogadishu fills at the weekend. Go with local advice, always.
  • The Camel MarketHargeisa's livestock market on a weekday morning, and the money that moves through it.
Places
  • Mogadishu and HargeisaA white Indian Ocean capital rebuilding, and a highland city that has been quiet for thirty years.
  • Coral Stone and Carved DoorsSwahili coral-rag architecture along the southern coast, with heavy carved doors.
  • Laas GeelRock shelters near Hargeisa painted with cattle and people, five thousand years old and unfaded.
  • Ports of the Indian OceanMogadishu, Merca and Barawa were trading cities when Europe had none on this ocean.
Ways to travel
  • Diving BerberaUnfished reefs in the Gulf of Aden, and almost no one to share them with.
  • Beginning to ExistNew hotels in Hargeisa and Mogadishu, mostly for business travellers, some of them good.
  • Ochre on RockLaas Geel in the morning, when the shelters are lit and the cattle look painted yesterday.
  • Berbera's Ottoman QuarterA decaying Ottoman and colonial old town by the harbour, being slowly repaired.
