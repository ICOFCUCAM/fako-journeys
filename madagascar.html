Madagascar

An island that evolved alone.

Separated from Africa 160 million years ago, with ninety per cent of its wildlife found nowhere else — lemurs, baobabs, the spiny forest of the south, and rainforest along the eastern escarpment.

What Madagascar is known for.

Nature & Landscapes

Six Ecosystems on One Island

Rainforest east, highlands centre, spiny forest south, dry deciduous west, and reef all round.

Wildlife

A Hundred Kinds of Lemur

A hundred and seven species and subspecies of lemur, every one of them endemic.

Mountains

The Highlands and Maromokotro

The central highlands run to two thousand metres; Maromokotro reaches 2,876 in the Tsaratanana.

Beaches & Coast

Nosy Be and the East Coast

Reef, whale sharks and humpbacks off Nosy Be, and long empty sand on the east coast.

Forests & Rainforest

Rainforest and Spiny Forest

Andasibe and Ranomafana in the east, and the spiny forest of the south which is like nowhere.

Wildlife
  • A Hundred Kinds of LemurA hundred and seven species and subspecies of lemur, every one of them endemic.
  • Not a Safari IslandNo lions and no elephant — the wildlife here is small, nocturnal, and worth the torchlight.
  • The Reason to Come CarefullyDeforestation is the country's crisis, and the parks that work are the ones the villages run.
  • Trek, Paddle, DiveRainforest trekking, the Tsiribihina by pirogue, and reef diving off Nosy Be.
People and culture
  • Malagasy, and Eighteen PeoplesOne language across the island, of Austronesian origin, and eighteen distinct peoples speaking it.
  • Famadihana and the AncestorsThe ancestors are present here in a way that shapes the calendar and the architecture.
  • Hira Gasy and DoniaHira gasy troupes in the highlands, and the Donia festival on Nosy Be in June.
  • Silk, Raffia and ZafimaniryWild silk lambas, raffia work, and the Zafimaniry woodcarving of the south-east.
  • Romazava and RiceRice three times a day, and romazava — beef and greens — with it.
  • Slow, and Worth ItRoads are slow and distances are long — but the children will hold a chameleon.
  • The Zebu MarketZebu cattle are wealth here, and the markets that trade them are the real calendar.
Places
  • AntananarivoA capital built up twelve hills, with stairs instead of streets in half of it.
  • Highland BrickTwo-storey red brick with a wooden balcony, and nothing else in Africa looks like it.
  • The Rova and the Royal HillsAmbohimanga, the royal hill of the Merina kings above Antananarivo.
  • Pirates and KingdomsÎle Sainte-Marie's pirate cemetery, and the Merina kingdom that unified the island.
Ways to travel
  • The RN7 and the TsingyThe road south through the highlands, and the limestone tsingy you cross on via ferrata.
  • Small LodgesOwner-run lodges at the park gates, and beach places on Nosy Be with a dozen rooms.
  • Baobabs at SixThe avenue at Morondava for twenty minutes at either end of the day, and nothing else like it.
  • MasoalaThe largest protected rainforest in the country, reached by boat, with whales in the bay.
