Botswana

A delta that never reaches the sea.

The Okavango empties into the Kalahari and evaporates, and the whole country is organised around that fact — low-volume, high-value safari, mokoro channels, and the salt pans of Makgadikgadi.

Nature & Landscapes

Delta, Pan and Desert

Water in the north, salt pans in the middle, and Kalahari sand for the rest of it.

Wildlife

The Largest Elephant Population

Chobe holds more elephant than anywhere on earth, and they come to the river at four.

Mountains

Tsodilo

Beaches & Coast

Landscapes

  • TsodiloNo real mountains — Tsodilo's four hills hold four thousand rock paintings instead.
  • No Falls, and the Zambezi Next DoorThe water here spreads rather than drops; Victoria Falls is ninety minutes from Kasane.
  • The Okavango and the ChobeA mokoro poled through papyrus channels is the correct speed for this country.
  • LandlockedThe nearest sea is a thousand kilometres away, and nobody here seems to mind.

Wildlife
  • The Largest Elephant PopulationChobe holds more elephant than anywhere on earth, and they come to the river at four.
  • Low Volume, High ValueFewer beds, larger concessions, and game drives that do not meet another vehicle.
  • Community TrustsConcessions leased through community trusts, so the villages hold the lease and the income.
  • Mokoro and WalkingPoling, walking and riding — this is the country where you get out of the vehicle.
People and culture
  • Batswana and SanSetswana across the country, and San communities in the Kalahari with the oldest culture on earth.
  • The Kalahari SanTracking with San guides, who will find in an hour what nobody else sees at all.
  • Maitisong and DithubarubaThe Maitisong festival in Gaborone, and the Kweneng cultural festival at Dithubaruba.
  • BasketryPalm baskets woven in the delta panhandle, dyed with bark, and collected worldwide.
  • Seswaa and BogobeSlow-pounded beef and sorghum porridge, and braai wherever there is a fire.
  • Not the Cheapest, but QuietNot a budget destination — but the children will not be sharing a sighting with ten vehicles.
  • Cattle PostsAway from the parks the country is cattle, and the cattle post is the centre of it.
Places
  • Maun and KasaneNot really cities — two safari towns with airstrips, which is what the country needs.
  • Tent, Deck and SandCamps built on decks above the flood, taken down when the concession lease ends.
  • Tsodilo HillsFour thousand paintings on four hills the San call the resting place of the gods.
  • Diamonds and DemocracyIndependent since 1966, and the one African country that used its diamonds well.
Ways to travel
  • Mokoro, Walking, HorsebackPoled canoes, walking safaris on the islands, and some of the best riding safaris anywhere.
  • The Concession CampsPrivate concessions the size of small countries, with six tents on them.
  • Water and LightAerial work over the delta, and reflections that only exist for the weeks of the flood.
  • The MakgadikgadiDrive onto the pan at night and there is no horizon and no sound at all.
