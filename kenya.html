25 ways to spend the time.

Landscapes
  • The Great Rift ValleyThe escarpment drops away below the Naivasha road, holding a chain of soda lakes and dormant volcanoes.
  • Mount Kenya, 5,199 mEquatorial glaciers, giant lobelia, and a trekking route to Point Lenana that needs no technical climbing.
  • Thomson's FallsThe Ewaso Ng'iro drops seventy-four metres into a fern gorge at Nyahururu, on the way north to Samburu.
  • Flamingo CountryNakuru, Bogoria and Elementaita hold soda water, hot springs and flamingo in numbers that change by the season.
  • The Swahili CoastDiani, Watamu and Lamu: white sand, warm Indian Ocean, and a coastline that traded with Arabia for a thousand years.
  • Kakamega, the Last of ItThe only Kenyan remnant of the Guineo-Congolian rainforest that once ran clear across the continent.
Wildlife
  • Wildlife on the SavannahThe Big Five, plus the reticulated giraffe and Grevy's zebra that live nowhere south of here.
  • The Great MigrationBetween July and October a million wildebeest cross the Mara river, and the crocodiles are already waiting.
  • Rhino, Counted DailyOl Pejeta and Lewa run some of Africa's most closely monitored rhino populations, funded by the beds on the property.
  • Walking Hell's GateThe one Kenyan park you can walk and cycle through, with gorge scrambling and geothermal steam.
People and culture
  • Maasai and SwahiliTwo Kenyas: the cattle-keeping highlands and the coral-stone, Arabic-inflected coast.
  • Samburu and MaasaiSamburu in the northern drylands, Maasai in the south, both still moving cattle between seasonal grazing.
  • Lamu Cultural FestivalDhow races, donkey races, henna and taarab music in the alleys of a town with almost no cars.
  • Beadwork and Kisii StoneMaasai beadwork, Kisii soapstone carving, and kiondo sisal baskets made the way they always were.
  • Nyama Choma and UgaliGoat roasted over charcoal, maize meal, sukuma wiki, and coastal biryani that came in by dhow.
  • The ConservanciesCommunity conservancies lease grazing land back for wildlife, and the lease pays the schools around the Mara.
  • Matatus and MarketsPainted, over-amplified matatus, Maasai Market on rotation through the week, and tea at every stop.
Places
  • Nairobi, Green City in the SunThe only capital on earth with a national park inside the city limits, and lions in sight of the skyline.
  • Coral Stone and Carved DoorsSwahili coral-rag houses with inner courtyards, and doors carved in Lamu and shipped up the coast.
  • Fort Jesus, MombasaPortuguese, built 1593, taken and retaken nine times — now UNESCO World Heritage above the old harbour.
  • Lamu Old TownThe oldest continuously inhabited Swahili settlement, carved doors, coral rag walls and no room for a car.
Ways to travel
  • Balloon Over the MaraUp before dawn, an hour over the plains at treetop height, and breakfast on the grass where you land.
  • Camps on the MaraSmall tented camps on private conservancies, night drives and off-road access the main reserve does not allow.
  • Kilimanjaro from AmboseliThe mountain is in Tanzania; the view of it is in Kenya, and it is only clear for an hour at each end of the day.
  • Chalbi and MarsabitThe desert north: Chalbi's salt pan, Marsabit's forested crater and the road down to Lake Turkana.
