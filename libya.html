People and culture
  • Arab, Berber, Tuareg, TebuFour peoples and three languages, and a coast that has been Phoenician, Greek, Roman, Ottoman and Italian.
  • The Tuareg and the TebuThe Tuareg in the southwest around Ghat, the Tebu in the Tibesti foothills in the southeast.
  • Ghat and the Desert FestivalsThe Ghat festival brings Tuareg from three countries for racing, music and poetry.
  • Silver and LeatherTuareg silver crosses, tooled leather from Ghadames, and Berber weaving in the Nafusa.
  • Bazin and BsisaBazin with lamb and paprika, couscous on Fridays, and shorba on the table all Ramadan.
  • Roman Cities Are Good for ThisLeptis is enormous, empty and safe to run around in, which not every ruin is.
  • Tea, Three TimesPoured from a height, three glasses, and no conversation is finished before the third.
Places
  • Tripoli and BenghaziAn Ottoman medina inside Italian arcades in Tripoli, and Benghazi rebuilding.
  • GhadamesA covered mud-brick town where the streets are tunnels and the roofs are the women's road.
  • Cyrene and SabrathaA Greek city on an escarpment above the sea, and a Roman theatre facing the water at Sabratha.
  • Five Sites, All of Them MajorLeptis, Sabratha, Cyrene, Ghadames and the Acacus rock art — every one on the World Heritage list.
Ways to travel
  • Into the AcacusFour-wheel-drive expeditions of a week, camping under the arches with Tuareg guides.
  • Thin on the GroundThere is very little at the top end here, and what exists is in Tripoli.
