An Afrinkong collection of places worth crossing a continent to see

Some places don’t need explaining. You just need to stand there.

From waterfalls that disappear into the earth to deserts with no end to them, from cities carved out of rock to forests where the last of something still lives — Africa holds places that change the scale of what you thought the world was.

23 places, and the list is ours — there is no official ranking of African wonders and this is not pretending to be one.
