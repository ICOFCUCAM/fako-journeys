01Highlights

What South Africa is known for.

Six of the twenty-seven, and the ones most people come for. The rest are below — and none of them is filler.

Nature & Landscapes

Fynbos and the Garden Route

The Cape Floral Kingdom is the smallest and richest of the six on earth, and most of it grows nowhere else.

Wildlife

The Big Five, Close

Kruger and the private Sabi Sands reserves beside it, where the leopards have been watched for generations.

Mountains

The Drakensberg

The Amphitheatre wall runs five kilometres and drops a kilometre, with San rock art in the caves beneath it.

Beaches & Coast

Two Oceans

Cold Atlantic on one side of the Cape peninsula, warm Indian on the other, forty minutes apart.

Forests & Rainforest

Tsitsikamma

Old yellowwood forest running down to a rocky coast, with the Storms River mouth cut through the middle.

Culture

Eleven Languages

Zulu, Xhosa, Afrikaans and eight more, plus the Cape Malay cooking that came with the slave trade.

03Why go

The case for South Africa.

Why Visit This Country

Two Weeks, Four Countries' Worth

Mountain, desert, reef, bush and two of Africa's most interesting cities, on roads you can drive yourself.

Hidden Gems

The Wild Coast and Karoo

Hole in the Wall and the Transkei green hills, and the Karoo's enormous silence in the other direction.

Eco Tourism

Marine and Rhino Work

Whale watching at Hermanus, penguins at Boulders, and rhino monitoring in the reserves that fund it.

Heritage

The Cradle of Humankind

The Sterkfontein caves have produced more early hominin fossils than any other site on the planet.
