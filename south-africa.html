02Experiences

25 ways to spend the time.

Every country we cover works through the same twenty-seven categories, so you can hold two of them side by side and compare like with like rather than one brochure against another. Compare South Africa with another → The two not listed here are the hero picture and the case for going, which has a section of its own below.

Landscapes
  • Fynbos and the Garden RouteThe Cape Floral Kingdom is the smallest and richest of the six on earth, and most of it grows nowhere else.
  • The DrakensbergThe Amphitheatre wall runs five kilometres and drops a kilometre, with San rock art in the caves beneath it.
  • Tugela, Off the AmphitheatreNearly a thousand metres in five leaps off the Drakensberg escarpment — among the highest falls on earth.
  • Blyde River CanyonThe third largest canyon in the world and the greenest, with the Three Rondavels standing in the middle of it.
  • Two OceansCold Atlantic on one side of the Cape peninsula, warm Indian on the other, forty minutes apart.
  • TsitsikammaOld yellowwood forest running down to a rocky coast, with the Storms River mouth cut through the middle.
Wildlife
  • The Big Five, CloseKruger and the private Sabi Sands reserves beside it, where the leopards have been watched for generations.
  • Sabi Sands, Off-RoadPrivate traverse means the vehicle can leave the road, which is why the sightings are what they are.
  • Marine and Rhino WorkWhale watching at Hermanus, penguins at Boulders, and rhino monitoring in the reserves that fund it.
  • Lion's Head at DawnNinety minutes up a spiral path with chains near the top, for the whole peninsula at sunrise.
People and culture
  • Eleven LanguagesZulu, Xhosa, Afrikaans and eight more, plus the Cape Malay cooking that came with the slave trade.
  • Zulu, Xhosa, NdebeleNdebele wall painting, Zulu beadwork with its own grammar, and Xhosa initiation still practised in the Eastern Cape.
  • Carnival and AfrikaBurnThe Cape Town Carnival in March, the Kaapse Klopse minstrels in January, and AfrikaBurn out in the Karoo.
  • Ndebele BeadworkBeadwork, wire sculpture and telephone-wire baskets, sold everywhere and made properly in very few places.
  • Braai, Bunny Chow, CurryFire is a national institution; Durban curry and Cape Malay bobotie came from two very different arrivals.
  • Township Tourism, Done RightGuided by residents, spending in the neighbourhood, and honest about what apartheid planning actually built.
  • Soweto, SundayVilakazi Street, the shisa nyama grills, and a township of a million people that runs at its own pace.
Places
  • Cape Town and JohannesburgOne built around a mountain and a harbour, the other on a gold reef, and nothing about them is alike.
  • Bo-Kaap and Cape DutchPainted Bo-Kaap terraces on the slope of Signal Hill, and gabled Cape Dutch homesteads out in the winelands.
  • Robben IslandTwelve kilometres off Cape Town, a political prison for three decades, now guided by the men who were held there.
  • The Cradle of HumankindThe Sterkfontein caves have produced more early hominin fossils than any other site on the planet.
Ways to travel
  • Bloukrans and the CageThe highest commercial bridge bungee in the world, and great white cage diving out of Gansbaai.
  • The WinelandsFranschhoek and Stellenbosch: Cape Dutch estates, serious kitchens, and a wine tram that means nobody drives.
  • Chapman's Peak DriveNine kilometres cut into the cliff between Hout Bay and Noordhoek, and every turnout is a photograph.
  • The Wild Coast and KarooHole in the Wall and the Transkei green hills, and the Karoo's enormous silence in the other direction.
